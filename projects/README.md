# Research project briefs

These briefs are starting points for the 180-hour research project of Module 33, which takes the place of the Technion's research project course (01240353). Each brief gives a question you can answer at home, the data or materials it needs, a method, a way to validate it, an hours budget and what it cannot show. Use one as it is, narrow it, or write your own in the same form with the [research proposal template](../templates/research-proposal.md). Lesson [33.1](../lessons/module-33/lesson-01.md) explains how to choose.

Be honest about one thing from the start: a real research project is supervised by a researcher who checks the question, the work and the report. Nobody supervises these. Plan to replace that as far as you can with a written proposal, a known-answer test, an hours log and an outside academic reader (Lesson [33.4](../lessons/module-33/lesson-04.md)). The finished project is a report written with the [lab report template](../templates/lab-report.md) as a base, plus an A0 poster (Lesson [33.3](../lessons/module-33/lesson-03.md)).

## Computational

- [Calculated versus measured IR spectra for 30 molecules](ir-benchmark/README.md) — how large is the error of a fast method and of DFT, and how much does scaling remove? Builds on Modules 20 and 31.
- [Colour of conjugated dyes: particle in a box against TD-DFT](dye-colour-tddft/README.md) — does a calculation beat the simple box for cyanines and polyenes? Builds on 30.4 and 31.6.

## Data

- [Predicting aqueous solubility from structure](solubility-model/README.md) — how much better than a four-descriptor equation can a model do on held-out compounds? Builds on Modules 5, 9 and 13.
- [Hammett constants from the IUPAC pKa compilation](hammett-pka/README.md) — do substituted benzoic acids in a large modern data set reproduce the textbook Hammett σ values? Builds on 28.3 and 09.4.

## Safe home measurement

- [Is supermarket vinegar what the label says?](vinegar-survey/README.md) — a survey of brands by titration with a full uncertainty budget. Builds on 10.6.
- [Temperature and the fading of food dye in bleach](dye-fading-temperature/README.md) — an activation energy from a phone camera. Builds on 23.4 and 22.4.
- [Speed of sound and the heat-capacity ratio of air](sound-gamma/README.md) — does the measured speed follow the ideal-gas prediction from cold to warm air? Builds on 30.3.

## Before you start

- Every home brief uses only the course's safe kitchen and pharmacy materials (Lesson [01.4](../lessons/module-01/lesson-04.md)). Do not add reagents, heat flammable liquids, or change a procedure in a way that creates a new hazard.
- Check the licence of every data source on the day you download it, and record it in your data dictionary (Lesson [33.2](../lessons/module-33/lesson-02.md)).
- Link back to the methods you learned: error analysis in Module 9 ([09.3](../lessons/module-09/lesson-03.md)), reading and writing papers in Module 11 ([11.1](../lessons/module-11/lesson-01.md), [11.3](../lessons/module-11/lesson-03.md)), and the computational tools of Module 31 ([31.1](../lessons/module-31/lesson-01.md)).
- The hours budgets assume the split of Lesson 33.1: reading 25, setup and pilot 20, data 50, analysis 35, report 30, poster 10, feedback 10.
