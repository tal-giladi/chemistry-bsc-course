# Module 7 quiz — Foundations of Chemistry A: Atoms, Bonds and Amounts

This quiz checks whether you can use the foundations of chemistry to make decisions: reading isotope patterns and amounts, explaining periodic trends, drawing and judging Lewis structures, predicting shapes, polarity and boiling points, finding limiting reagents and yields, deciding when a gas is ideal, and preparing solutions with a known uncertainty. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70 %.

Review these lessons first:

- [07.1 · Atoms, isotopes and the mole](../lessons/module-07/lesson-01.md)
- [07.2 · Electron configuration and periodic trends](../lessons/module-07/lesson-02.md)
- [07.3 · Ionic and covalent bonding, Lewis structures](../lessons/module-07/lesson-03.md)
- [07.4 · VSEPR, polarity and intermolecular forces](../lessons/module-07/lesson-04.md)
- [07.5 · Stoichiometry and limiting reagents](../lessons/module-07/lesson-05.md)
- [07.6 · Gases: ideal and real](../lessons/module-07/lesson-06.md)
- [07.7 · Lab: weighing, glassware and making solutions](../lessons/module-07/lesson-07.md)

Have a calculator ready. Several questions give numbers; the wrong options are the answers that common slips produce.
