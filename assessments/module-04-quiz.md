# Module 4 quiz — Physics for Chemists

This quiz checks whether you can use physics to make decisions about molecules and measurements: comparing bond and thermal energies, reading a force from an energy curve, predicting a vibrational frequency and its isotope shift, judging how a solvent changes ionic attraction, reasoning about a circuit and a meter, and choosing light of the right energy. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70 %.

Review these lessons first:

- [04.1 · Forces, energy and momentum](../lessons/module-04/lesson-01.md)
- [04.2 · Oscillations and waves](../lessons/module-04/lesson-02.md)
- [04.3 · Electrostatics: charge, field and potential](../lessons/module-04/lesson-03.md)
- [04.4 · Circuits, current and resistance](../lessons/module-04/lesson-04.md)
- [04.5 · Light as an electromagnetic wave and as photons](../lessons/module-04/lesson-05.md)

Have a calculator ready. Several questions give numbers; the wrong options are the answers that common slips produce.
