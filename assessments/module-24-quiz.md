# Module 24 quiz — Organic Chemistry Lab 1

This quiz checks whether you can think like a student at a real organic bench, using what you practised on the virtual bench: plan a reflux and a work-up, keep the right layer, purify a solid or a liquid, prove purity with TLC and a melting point, run the aspirin, SN2, Grignard and aldol teaching syntheses, and carry a multi-step sequence through with a check at every step. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70 %.

Review these lessons first:

- [24.1 · Lab techniques: reflux, extraction and drying](../lessons/module-24/lesson-01.md)
- [24.2 · Purification: recrystallisation and distillation](../lessons/module-24/lesson-02.md)
- [24.3 · Checking purity: TLC and melting point](../lessons/module-24/lesson-03.md)
- [24.4 · Virtual synthesis: aspirin](../lessons/module-24/lesson-04.md)
- [24.5 · Virtual synthesis: substitution, Grignard and aldol](../lessons/module-24/lesson-05.md)
- [24.6 · Multi-step synthesis with spectra at every step](../lessons/module-24/lesson-06.md)

Have a calculator ready. A few questions need a short calculation (a yield, a recovery or a distribution ratio); the wrong options are the answers that common slips produce, such as picking the wrong limiting reagent, adding yields instead of multiplying them, trusting the position of a layer instead of its density, or reading a high crude yield as success.
