# Module 19 quiz — Analytical Chemistry 2: Instrumental Methods

This quiz checks whether you can choose and run an instrumental method and judge its results: planning UV-Vis measurements around stray light and photometric error, using fluorescence and atomic spectroscopy without falling into quenching or interference traps, designing extractions, reading chromatograms with plates, selectivity and resolution, choosing GC or HPLC settings and identifying peaks, and diagnosing a cyclic voltammogram. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70 %.

Review these lessons first:

- [19.1 · UV-Vis spectrophotometry and Beer-Lambert](../lessons/module-19/lesson-01.md)
- [19.2 · Fluorescence and atomic spectroscopy](../lessons/module-19/lesson-02.md)
- [19.3 · Extraction and the theory of chromatography](../lessons/module-19/lesson-03.md)
- [19.4 · Gas and liquid chromatography](../lessons/module-19/lesson-04.md)
- [19.5 · Electroanalysis and cyclic voltammetry](../lessons/module-19/lesson-05.md)

Keep a calculator at hand. Most questions describe a lab situation and ask what you would do next; the wrong options are the choices an analyst makes when one step of the reasoning is skipped.
