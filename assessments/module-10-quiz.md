# Module 10 quiz — Analytical Chemistry Lab 1

This quiz checks whether you can run and judge the classic wet-chemistry methods of an analytical lab: choose an indicator and standardise a titrant, find an end point from electrode data, pick the right precipitation or EDTA method for a sample, plan a gravimetric or coulometric measurement, calibrate an ion-selective electrode, and turn repeated titrations into a result with an honest uncertainty. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70 %.

- [10.3 · Precipitation and complexometric titrations](../lessons/module-10/lesson-03.md)
