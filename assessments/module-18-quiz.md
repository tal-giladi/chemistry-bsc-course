# Module 18 quiz — Inorganic Chemistry

This quiz checks whether you can reason like an inorganic chemist with this module's tools: estimate how strongly an atom holds its electrons, decide whether an ionic compound can exist from a lattice-energy estimate, read the formula of a coordination compound, count its isomers, predict its spin state and explain its colour and magnetic moment. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70 %.

Review these lessons first:

- [18.1 · Periodic trends in depth and the main-group elements](../lessons/module-18/lesson-01.md)
- [18.2 · Solid structures and lattice energy](../lessons/module-18/lesson-02.md)
- [18.3 · Coordination compounds: geometry and isomers](../lessons/module-18/lesson-03.md)
- [18.4 · Crystal field and ligand field theory](../lessons/module-18/lesson-04.md)
- [18.5 · Colour and magnetism of transition-metal complexes](../lessons/module-18/lesson-05.md)

Have paper and a calculator ready. A few questions need a short calculation (a Born–Haber step, a d-electron count, a wavelength-to-wavenumber conversion or a spin-only moment); the wrong options are the answers that common slips produce, such as removing 3d electrons before 4s, mixing up the absorbed and the seen colour, or comparing the splitting with the free-ion pairing energy.
