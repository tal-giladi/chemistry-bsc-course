# Module 3 quiz — Linear Algebra and Differential Equations

This quiz checks whether you can use linear algebra and differential equations to make decisions with chemical data: setting up and judging a linear system, reading eigenvalues as energies or frequencies, choosing the right rate law and solver, analysing an oscillator, and handling coupled reactions. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70 %.

Review these lessons first:

- [03.1 · Vectors, matrices and solving linear systems](../lessons/module-03/lesson-01.md)
- [03.2 · Eigenvalues and eigenvectors](../lessons/module-03/lesson-02.md)
- [03.3 · First-order differential equations and rate laws](../lessons/module-03/lesson-03.md)
- [03.4 · Second-order equations and oscillators](../lessons/module-03/lesson-04.md)
- [03.5 · Coupled systems: consecutive reactions](../lessons/module-03/lesson-05.md)

Have a calculator ready. Several questions give numbers; the wrong options are the answers that common slips produce.
