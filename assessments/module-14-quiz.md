# Module 14 quiz — Chemical Physics Lab: Oscillators and Signals

This quiz checks whether you can run and interpret the measurements of the chemical physics lab: comparing static and dynamic force constants, reading damping and Q from a ringdown, recognising anharmonicity, predicting normal modes of coupled masses and of CO2, choosing a sample rate and record length for a Fourier transform, and fitting a resonance curve. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70 %.

- [14.3 · Normal modes of three coupled masses](../lessons/module-14/lesson-03.md)
- [14.4 · From coupled masses to the vibrations of CO2](../lessons/module-14/lesson-04.md)
- [14.5 · Signals, sampling and the Fourier transform](../lessons/module-14/lesson-05.md)
