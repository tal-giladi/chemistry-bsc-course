# Module 2 quiz — Calculus for Chemists

This quiz checks whether you can use calculus to make decisions with chemical data: choosing the plot that straightens a law, getting a rate from measurements, integrating to find work or heat, judging when an approximation is safe, and finding which measurement limits a result. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70 %.

Review these lessons first:

- [02.1 · Functions chemists meet: power, exponential and logarithm](../lessons/module-02/lesson-01.md)
- [02.2 · Derivatives as rates of change](../lessons/module-02/lesson-02.md)
- [02.3 · Integrals as accumulated change](../lessons/module-02/lesson-03.md)
- [02.4 · Series and approximations](../lessons/module-02/lesson-04.md)
- [02.5 · Functions of several variables and partial derivatives](../lessons/module-02/lesson-05.md)

Have a calculator ready. Several questions give numbers; the wrong options are the answers that common slips produce.
