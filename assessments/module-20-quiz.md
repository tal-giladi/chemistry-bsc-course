# Module 20 quiz — Molecular Spectroscopy and Symmetry

This quiz checks whether you can reason like a spectroscopist with this module's tools: decide which spectra a molecule will show, pull rotational constants and bond lengths out of real line positions, read the shape of a vibrational or electronic band, assign a point group and predict how many IR and Raman bands a molecule has. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70 %.

Review these lessons first:

- [20.1 · Light-matter interaction and selection rules](../lessons/module-20/lesson-01.md)
- [20.2 · Rotational spectra](../lessons/module-20/lesson-02.md)
- [20.3 · Vibrational spectra and Raman](../lessons/module-20/lesson-03.md)
- [20.4 · Electronic spectra and Franck-Condon](../lessons/module-20/lesson-04.md)
- [20.5 · Symmetry elements and point groups](../lessons/module-20/lesson-05.md)
- [20.6 · Character tables and predicting IR and Raman bands](../lessons/module-20/lesson-06.md)

Have paper and a calculator ready. A few questions need a short calculation (a rotational constant from line positions, a combination difference, a population maximum or a reduction-formula result); the wrong options are the answers that common slips produce, such as confusing level and line spacings, forgetting the degeneracy, using the wrong pair of lines or forgetting to remove translations and rotations.
