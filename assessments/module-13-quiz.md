# Module 13 quiz — Chemical Thermodynamics

This quiz checks whether you can use thermodynamics to make decisions with real data: keep the signs of heat and work straight, correct a bomb-calorimeter result, move a reaction enthalpy and an equilibrium constant to the temperature where a process actually runs, judge whether a process is reversible, read and extend a phase diagram, and allow for non-ideal solutions. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70 %.

Review these lessons first:

- [13.1 · Systems, state functions and the first law](../lessons/module-13/lesson-01.md)
- [13.2 · Enthalpy, heat capacity and calorimetry](../lessons/module-13/lesson-02.md)
- [13.3 · The second law and entropy](../lessons/module-13/lesson-03.md)
- [13.4 · Free energy and chemical potential](../lessons/module-13/lesson-04.md)
- [13.5 · Phase equilibria and phase diagrams](../lessons/module-13/lesson-05.md)
- [13.6 · Solutions, activity and equilibrium constants](../lessons/module-13/lesson-06.md)

Have a calculator ready ($R$ = 8.314 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹; $RT$ = 2.479 kJ/mol at 298 K). Several questions give numbers; the wrong options are the answers that common slips produce, such as Celsius instead of kelvin, J mixed with kJ, or a forgotten stoichiometric factor.
