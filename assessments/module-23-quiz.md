# Module 23 quiz — Physical Chemistry Lab 1

This quiz checks whether you can run and judge physical chemistry experiments: choose conditions that make the analysis valid, turn raw readings into a result with an uncertainty, spot the systematic error that matters most, and keep a home experiment safe. It has 9 scenario questions covering gases, calorimetry, spectrophotometric equilibrium, kinetics, cells and surface tension. The pass mark is 70 %.

Review these lessons first:

- [23.1 · Gas laws and kinetic theory in the lab](../lessons/module-23/lesson-01.md)
- [23.2 · Thermochemistry: enthalpy of neutralisation and solution](../lessons/module-23/lesson-02.md)
- [23.3 · Equilibrium constant by spectrophotometry](../lessons/module-23/lesson-03.md)
- [23.4 · Kinetics: dye fading tracked with a phone camera](../lessons/module-23/lesson-04.md)
- [23.5 · Electrochemistry: cells, potentials and a lemon battery](../lessons/module-23/lesson-05.md)
- [23.6 · Surface tension and detergents](../lessons/module-23/lesson-06.md)

Have a calculator ready ($R$ = 8.314 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹; $2.303RT/F$ = 59.16 mV at 25 °C; water's surface tension 72.6 mN/m at 21 °C). The wrong options are the answers that common slips produce, such as an unextrapolated temperature, a forgotten offset, the wrong ionic factor or a one-electron Nernst slope.
