# Module 8 quiz — Foundations of Chemistry B

This quiz checks whether you can use energy, equilibrium and electrons to make decisions: predict heat from a table, decide whether a process runs and at what temperature, shift an equilibrium on purpose, work out a pH, balance and use a redox reaction, read a cell voltage, and judge a calorimetry or titration result. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70 %.

Review these lessons first:

- [08.1 · Thermochemistry and Hess's law](../lessons/module-08/lesson-01.md)
- [08.2 · Entropy, free energy and spontaneity](../lessons/module-08/lesson-02.md)
- [08.3 · Chemical equilibrium and Le Chatelier](../lessons/module-08/lesson-03.md)
- [08.4 · Acids, bases and pH](../lessons/module-08/lesson-04.md)
- [08.5 · Redox reactions and balancing](../lessons/module-08/lesson-05.md)
- [08.6 · Electrochemical cells and the Nernst equation](../lessons/module-08/lesson-06.md)
- [08.7 · Lab: coffee-cup calorimetry and a first titration](../lessons/module-08/lesson-07.md)

Have a calculator ready. Several questions give numbers; the wrong options are the answers that common slips produce.
