# Module 1 quiz

This quiz checks the whole of Module 1 with realistic situations: planning your study, units and significant figures, keeping a notebook and writing a report, reading hazard labels and the Israeli rules for home work, and judging a home measurement with its uncertainty. Most questions ask you to make a decision, not to recall a sentence.

There are 9 multiple-choice questions. The pass mark is 70%. You may use a calculator.
