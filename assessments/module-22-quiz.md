# Module 22 quiz — Chemical Kinetics

This quiz checks whether you can reason like a kineticist with this module's tools: define a rate and find an order from real measurements, pick a method that fits the timescale, turn a mechanism into a rate law with the steady-state approximation, read activation energies and entropies of activation, and analyse catalysed and enzyme reactions. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70 %.

Review these lessons first:

- [22.1 · Rate laws and integrated rate laws](../lessons/module-22/lesson-01.md)
- [22.2 · Measuring rates: methods and data analysis](../lessons/module-22/lesson-02.md)
- [22.3 · Mechanisms and the steady-state approximation](../lessons/module-22/lesson-03.md)
- [22.4 · Temperature dependence: Arrhenius and transition-state theory](../lessons/module-22/lesson-04.md)
- [22.5 · Catalysis and enzyme kinetics](../lessons/module-22/lesson-05.md)

Have paper and a calculator ready. A few questions need a short calculation (a lifetime, a relaxation time or a Michaelis–Menten rate); the wrong options are the answers that common slips produce, such as using $k_f$ instead of $k_f + k_r$, reading orders from coefficients, trusting a straight line outside its range or confusing $k_\text{cat}$ with $k_\text{cat}/K_m$.
