# Module 6 quiz — Biology for Chemists

This quiz checks whether you can use chemistry to reason about living cells: diffusion and cell size, what crosses a membrane, osmosis and membrane potentials, the charge and hydrogen bonds of proteins, base pairing and the genetic code, and the free-energy budget of ATP. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70 %.

Review these lessons first:

- [06.1 · Cells, membranes and compartments](../lessons/module-06/lesson-01.md)
- [06.2 · Proteins: from amino acids to folds](../lessons/module-06/lesson-02.md)
- [06.3 · Nucleic acids and the flow of information](../lessons/module-06/lesson-03.md)
- [06.4 · Metabolism and energy currency](../lessons/module-06/lesson-04.md)

Have a calculator ready. Several questions give numbers; the wrong options are the answers that common slips produce.
