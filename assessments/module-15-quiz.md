# Module 15 quiz — Organic Chemistry 1: Structure and Reactions

This quiz checks whether you can use the ideas of Module 15 to make decisions about real molecules and reactions: reading hybridisation and functional groups from a structure, predicting which conformation or chair dominates, assigning and comparing stereoisomers, judging proton transfers from pKa values, choosing between SN1, SN2, E1 and E2, and predicting the products of additions to alkenes and alkynes. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70 %.

Review these lessons first:

- [15.1 · Bonding, hybridisation and functional groups](../lessons/module-15/lesson-01.md)
- [15.2 · Conformations and strain](../lessons/module-15/lesson-02.md)
- [15.3 · Stereochemistry and chirality](../lessons/module-15/lesson-03.md)
- [15.4 · Curved arrows, acids and bases in organic chemistry](../lessons/module-15/lesson-04.md)
- [15.5 · Nucleophilic substitution: SN1 and SN2](../lessons/module-15/lesson-05.md)
- [15.6 · Elimination: E1 and E2](../lessons/module-15/lesson-06.md)
- [15.7 · Alkenes and alkynes](../lessons/module-15/lesson-07.md)

Have a pKa table and a pencil ready. Several questions ask you to follow a reaction through two steps; the wrong options are the products that common slips produce.
