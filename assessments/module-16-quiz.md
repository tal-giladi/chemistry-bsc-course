# Module 16 quiz — Organic Chemistry 2: Carbonyls, Aromatics and Synthesis

This quiz checks whether you can make the decisions a synthetic chemist makes with this module's reactions: pick an oxidant or reductant that touches only the group you mean, push an equilibrium to a useful yield, choose a base for an enolate, put substituents on a benzene ring in the right order, judge whether a nitrogen is charged at body pH, and plan a short route. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70 %.

Review these lessons first:

- [16.1 · Alcohols, ethers and oxidation](../lessons/module-16/lesson-01.md)
- [16.2 · Aldehydes, ketones and nucleophilic addition](../lessons/module-16/lesson-02.md)
- [16.3 · Carboxylic acids and their derivatives](../lessons/module-16/lesson-03.md)
- [16.4 · Enolates and the aldol reaction](../lessons/module-16/lesson-04.md)
- [16.5 · Aromaticity and electrophilic aromatic substitution](../lessons/module-16/lesson-05.md)
- [16.6 · Amines, sugars and heterocycles](../lessons/module-16/lesson-06.md)
- [16.7 · Retrosynthesis and multi-step planning](../lessons/module-16/lesson-07.md)

Have paper ready to draw structures. A few questions need a short calculation with $\mathrm{p}K_a$ values or yields; the wrong options are the answers that common slips produce, such as the wrong directing effect, a reagent that also attacks a second group, or the total step count used in place of the longest linear sequence.
