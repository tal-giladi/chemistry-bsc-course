# Module 32 quiz — Electives: Electrochemistry and Solid State

This quiz checks whether you can make the decisions of the two electives in this module: read electrode kinetics from a Tafel plot and correct it for resistance, judge a battery or a corrosion problem with Faraday's law and mixed-potential theory, index a powder pattern without being fooled by Kβ lines or zero shifts, and use band theory to choose a semiconductor. It has 9 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70 %.

Review these lessons first:

- [32.1 · Electrode kinetics and the Butler-Volmer equation](../lessons/module-32/lesson-01.md)
- [32.2 · Batteries, fuel cells and corrosion](../lessons/module-32/lesson-02.md)
- [32.3 · Crystals, lattices and X-ray diffraction](../lessons/module-32/lesson-03.md)
- [32.4 · Band theory: metals, semiconductors and insulators](../lessons/module-32/lesson-04.md)
- [32.5 · Choosing your further electives](../lessons/module-32/lesson-05.md)

Have a calculator ready ($F$ = 96485 C/mol; $RT/F$ = 25.69 mV and $2.303RT/F$ = 59.2 mV at 25 °C; $k_B$ = 8.617 × 10⁻⁵ eV/K; Cu Kα₁ = 1.5406 Å; ASTM G102: CR (mm/y) = 3.27 × 10⁻³ × $i_\text{corr}$ (µA/cm²) × EW / ρ). The wrong options are the answers that common slips produce, such as skipping the iR correction, using ln instead of log, forgetting the factor 2 in $E_g/2kT$, or calling a Kβ line a new phase.
