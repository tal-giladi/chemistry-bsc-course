# Module 12 quiz — Quantum Theory in Chemistry

This quiz checks whether you can use quantum theory to make decisions about real atoms, molecules and spectra: reading line spectra, judging when quantum effects matter, applying the particle in a box to dyes, estimating tunnelling, using vibrational and rotational constants, reasoning with hydrogen-like orbitals, molecular orbitals and selection rules, and trusting (or not trusting) a numerical solver. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70 %.

Review these lessons first:

- [12.1 · Why classical physics fails: photons, spectra and waves](../lessons/module-12/lesson-01.md)
- [12.2 · The Schrödinger equation and the particle in a box](../lessons/module-12/lesson-02.md)
- [12.3 · Tunnelling](../lessons/module-12/lesson-03.md)
- [12.4 · The harmonic oscillator and molecular vibrations](../lessons/module-12/lesson-04.md)
- [12.5 · Rotors, angular momentum and the hydrogen atom](../lessons/module-12/lesson-05.md)
- [12.6 · Many-electron atoms, molecular orbitals and selection rules](../lessons/module-12/lesson-06.md)

Have a calculator ready. Several questions give numbers; the wrong options are the answers that common slips produce.
