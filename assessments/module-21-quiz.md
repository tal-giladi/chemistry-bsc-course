# Module 21 quiz — Statistical Thermodynamics

This quiz checks whether you can go from energy levels to thermodynamics and back: decide which levels are populated, read a partition function, explain a heat capacity or an entropy from molecular data, spot a missing symmetry number or degeneracy, and judge an equilibrium constant calculated from spectroscopic constants. It has 9 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70 %.

Review these lessons first:

- [21.1 · Microstates, the Boltzmann distribution and temperature](../lessons/module-21/lesson-01.md)
- [21.2 · The partition function](../lessons/module-21/lesson-02.md)
- [21.3 · Molecular partition functions: translation, rotation, vibration](../lessons/module-21/lesson-03.md)
- [21.4 · From partition functions to equilibrium constants](../lessons/module-21/lesson-04.md)

Have a calculator ready ($hc/k_B$ = 1.4388 cm K; $k_BT/hc$ = 207 cm⁻¹ at 298 K; $R$ = 8.314 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹). The wrong options are the answers that common slips produce, such as a forgotten degeneracy or symmetry number, $D_e$ in place of $D_0$, or levels measured from different zeros.
