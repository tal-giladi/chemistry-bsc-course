# Module 30 quiz — Physical Chemistry Lab 2

This quiz checks whether you can plan and judge advanced physical chemistry measurements: choose the resolution or frequency that makes a spectrum interpretable, assign lines and hyperfine patterns, design an experiment so that a correction falls out of the fit, carry uncertainties through to a result, and decide whether a model has really been tested. It has 9 scenario questions covering the HCl rotation-vibration spectrum, ESR hyperfine splitting, the speed of sound and γ of air, and conjugated dyes. The pass mark is 70 %.

Review these lessons first:

- [30.1 · IR spectroscopy from first principles: HCl rotation-vibration](../lessons/module-30/lesson-01.md)
- [30.2 · Electron spin resonance and hyperfine splitting](../lessons/module-30/lesson-02.md)
- [30.3 · Speed of sound and the heat-capacity ratio of air](../lessons/module-30/lesson-03.md)
- [30.4 · Electronic spectra of conjugated dyes: particle in a box](../lessons/module-30/lesson-04.md)

Have a calculator ready ($R$ = 8.314 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹; $h/\mu_B$ = 7.145 × 10⁻¹¹ T s; $8m_ec\ell^2/h$ = 63.7 nm for ℓ = 0.139 nm). The wrong options are the answers that common slips produce, such as a forgotten end correction, a first-order formula used outside its range, or a fit mistaken for a test.
