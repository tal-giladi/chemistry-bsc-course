# Module 11 quiz — Scientific English: Reading and Writing Chemistry

This quiz checks whether you can make the decisions a chemist makes when reading and writing: deciding quickly whether a paper is worth reading, checking a paper against its own numbers and criteria, choosing which source of a value to cite, spotting an invented or retracted reference, judging a journal and a claim by its evidence, writing a result with its uncertainty and the right strength of claim, and choosing a figure that shows the data honestly. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70 %.

Review these lessons first:

- [11.1 · Reading a research paper efficiently](../lessons/module-11/lesson-01.md)
- [11.2 · Finding and judging sources and data](../lessons/module-11/lesson-02.md)
- [11.3 · Writing a lab report and a figure that tells the story](../lessons/module-11/lesson-03.md)

A calculator helps for two questions. The wrong options are the choices that a hurried reader or writer typically makes.
