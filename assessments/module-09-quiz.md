# Module 9 quiz — Analytical Chemistry 1: Measurement and Error

This quiz checks whether you can make the decisions an analyst makes with real data: telling random from systematic error and finding bias with a reference material, reporting a mean with its confidence interval and choosing the right significance test, propagating uncertainty and reading a budget, designing a calibration and reporting results near the detection limit, predicting titration curves and choosing an indicator, and sizing a buffer or a gravimetric procedure. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70 %.

Review these lessons first:

- [09.1 · Types of error, accuracy and precision](../lessons/module-09/lesson-01.md)
- [09.2 · Statistics: mean, standard deviation and confidence intervals](../lessons/module-09/lesson-02.md)
- [09.3 · Propagation of uncertainty](../lessons/module-09/lesson-03.md)
- [09.4 · Calibration, linear regression and detection limits](../lessons/module-09/lesson-04.md)
- [09.5 · Titration theory and titration curves](../lessons/module-09/lesson-05.md)
- [09.6 · Buffers and gravimetric analysis](../lessons/module-09/lesson-06.md)

Have a calculator ready. Several questions give numbers; the wrong options are the answers that common slips produce.
