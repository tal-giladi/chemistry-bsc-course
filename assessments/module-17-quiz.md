# Module 17 quiz — Identifying Compounds from Spectra

This quiz checks whether you can combine mass spectra, IR spectra and ¹H and ¹³C NMR spectra to decide what a compound is: finding the molecular ion and the formula, reading isotope patterns and fragments, naming functional groups from IR bands, counting and placing NMR signals, reading splitting patterns, and choosing between isomers with the one observation that decides. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70 %.

Review these lessons first:

- [17.1 · Mass spectrometry: molecular ion and fragments](../lessons/module-17/lesson-01.md)
- [17.2 · Infrared spectroscopy: functional groups](../lessons/module-17/lesson-02.md)
- [17.3 · Proton NMR: shift, integration and splitting](../lessons/module-17/lesson-03.md)
- [17.4 · Carbon NMR and 2D NMR basics](../lessons/module-17/lesson-04.md)
- [17.5 · Putting it together: solving unknowns](../lessons/module-17/lesson-05.md)
- [17.6 · Project: 50 unknowns from real SDBS spectra](../lessons/module-17/lesson-06.md)

Keep a pencil and the DBE formula at hand. Several questions give you data from two or three spectra at once; the wrong options are the structures that fit only part of the data.
