# Instructor notes — 01.1 How this course works and what it cannot train

- **Objective:** the learner knows the lesson anatomy, studies with self-testing and spaced review, can budget time, and can state the four things the course does not train.
- **Likely confusion:** "practice minutes" sounds optional. Stress that the hands-on part is the lesson, and that external MIT problem sets are part of the course, not extras.
- **Misconception:** "if it feels familiar, I know it." Fluency from rereading is mistaken for knowledge. Ask the learner to explain a takeaway with the page closed.
- **Analogy:** reading a lesson is watching a swimming video; the hands-on is getting in the pool; spaced review is swimming again next week so you do not forget how.
- **Expected hands-on result:** skills check 7–8/8 for a learner ready to start; a calendar with 10–12 h blocks for four weeks and three review slots per lesson. Learners scoring 4 or fewer should do a week of algebra revision before Module 2.
- **Extension:** read the scope list in `plan.md` ("What this plan does not train") and look up one Technion lab course in the catalog; compare its description with the simulation that replaces it later in the course.
