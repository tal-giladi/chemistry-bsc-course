# Instructor notes — 31.6 Excited states with time-dependent DFT

- Objective: the learner reads TD-DFT output (vertical energies, oscillator strengths, dark states), chooses functional, basis, roots and TDA/full settings, compares a homologous series with measured λmax, and completes the module practical: calculated versus measured IR for six molecules with scaling and an error analysis in a lab report.
- Likely confusion: vertical energy versus band maximum versus 0–0 energy; why the lowest state is not the band; TDA being ORCA's default.
- Misconception: "a functional that matches one number is the better functional" (B3LYP lands closer than CAM-B3LYP for these polyenes partly because of the gas-versus-solution comparison). Second, in the practical: "scaling always improves every molecule" (GFN2-xTB water gets worse, 69 → 118 cm⁻¹).
- Analogy: the oscillator strength is the volume knob of each state; TD-DFT tells you where every station is on the dial and how loud it plays, and the band you hear is the loud one.
- Expected hands-on result: xtb geometries lowest modes 145.1, 86.6, 54.9, 1085.6 cm⁻¹, no imaginary. Reference (PySCF 2.14, def2-SVP): CAM-B3LYP full 6.225/5.098/4.383 eV (f 0.72/1.12/1.55), TDA 6.727/5.526/4.756, B3LYP full 6.062/4.888/4.148; measured 217/258/290 nm = 5.71/4.81/4.28 eV; formaldehyde CAM TDA dark 4.095 eV f = 0.000, bright 8.712 eV f = 0.176. Practical (GFN2-xTB): 32 modes, s = 0.9824, RMS 94 → 85 cm⁻¹; per molecule scaled H2O 118, NH3 87, CH4 54, CO2 117, H2CO 60, HCN 100; worst CO2 2349 (2546), NH3 950 (1139), H2O 3756 (3586). ORCA TD-DFT and r2SCAN-3c practical runs were not made by the author.
