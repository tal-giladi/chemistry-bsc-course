# Instructor notes — 07.1 Atoms, isotopes and the mole

- **Objective:** Z, N, A from a symbol; atomic weight as a weighted mean; mass–mole–particle conversions; binomial isotope patterns.
- **Likely confusion:** atomic weight (dimensionless, relative to ¹²C/12) vs molar mass (g/mol) vs isotope mass (Da). Numerically equal, different meanings.
- **Misconception:** "the periodic table mass is the mass of one atom". No atom of chlorine weighs 35.45 Da; it is an average of 34.97 and 36.97.
- **Analogy:** the atomic weight is like the average grade of a class with two kinds of students. Nobody scored the average, but it is what you get when you weigh the whole class at once.
- **Expected hands-on result:** Part A 4–5/5; problem 5 is often answered "1 : 1 : 1" (missing the factor 2). Python: Cl 35.4529, Mg 24.3051, Cu 63.5460, Br 79.9035; Cl₂ 100 : 64 : 10.
- **Extension:** ask for the pattern of C₂Cl₄ (tetrachloroethene) and compare with the NIST WebBook mass spectrum; then explain the ¹³C M+1 peak of a C₂₀ compound (about 22%).
