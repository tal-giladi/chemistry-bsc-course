# Instructor notes — 07.3 Ionic and covalent bonding, Lewis structures

- **Objective:** bond type from Δχ; correct Lewis structures with formal charges; best structure and resonance; the three octet exceptions.
- **Likely confusion:** formal charge vs oxidation state vs real partial charge. Formal charge assumes equal sharing; oxidation state assumes total transfer; the real charge is in between.
- **Misconception:** resonance as rapid flipping between structures. Stress the hybrid; equal bond lengths are the evidence.
- **Analogy:** a rhinoceros described to someone who has only seen a dragon and a unicorn: "partly like each". The animal is real and single; the descriptions are the limit of what you have to describe it with.
- **Expected hands-on result:** 8–10/10. Common slips: CO₃²⁻ with 22 electrons, O₃ drawn as a triangle, NO₂ forced into an octet, SF₆ "impossible".
- **Extension:** draw SO₄²⁻ two ways (all single bonds with S +2, or two S=O with all FC zero on S) and discuss why modern computations favour the charged, octet structure; the formal-charge rule is a heuristic.
