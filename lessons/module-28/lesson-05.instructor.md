# Instructor notes — 28.5 Probing mechanisms: isotope effects and trapping (module practical)

- Objective: the learner estimates a primary KIE from ZPE, reads kH/kD values (primary versus secondary, ≈1), chooses trapping/clock/crossover/stereochemical probes, computes a trap rate constant from a radical clock, and runs the module practical: Hammett σp versus σp+ with residuals, Yukawa–Tsuno with parameter correlation, KIE, and a reasoned energy profile for the 3-aminothiophene + ArN₂⁺ coupling.
- Likely confusion: k versus k₁ in the practical (protonated amine does not react: k₁ = k(1 + [H₃O⁺]/Ka)); substituent on the electrophile, not on the nucleophile, so ρ > 0 means faster with acceptors; ratio of products versus ratio of rate constants in the clock equation.
- Misconception: "no isotope effect means no C–H bond breaks" — it breaks, just not in the slow step. Second: "R² decides the σ scale" — residual patterns do.
- Analogy: a radical clock is a stopwatch that starts when the radical forms; the trap is the hand that stops it. The fraction caught before the clock "rings" (ring opening) times the clock's known speed gives the speed of the hand.
- Expected hands-on result: kH/kD 6.4/6.8 at 298 K and 4.4/4.7 at 373 K (2900/3000 cm⁻¹); k_T 3.0e8 M⁻¹ s⁻¹ (worked example lifetime 6.7 ns). Practical: recomputed k₁ within 2 % (CH₃ at pH 1: 57.8 against 58.8); σp ρ = 3.39 ± 0.16, R² 0.968, rms 0.22, p-OCH₃ residual −0.40; σp+ ρ = 2.48 ± 0.10, intercept 2.91, R² 0.976, rms 0.20 (matches the paper's Eq. 3; the paper quotes R² 0.9879, probably from a different data subset); Yukawa–Tsuno free fit ρ 2.92 ± 0.16, r 0.49 ± 0.14, correlation −0.81 against the paper's 2.11 and 1.54 (not reproducible from Table 1 alone — say so, it is a good lesson in honest reporting); kH/kD 0.96–1.16 (mean 1.07).
- Extension: fit pH 1 and pH 2 separately and compare ρ; constrain r = 1 (pure σ+) and r = 0 (pure σp) and compare χ² with the free fit; or draw the full mechanism with curved arrows (attack, σ-complex, deprotonation) and mark where the 2-deuterium label goes.
