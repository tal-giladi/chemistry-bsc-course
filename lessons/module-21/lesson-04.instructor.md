# Instructor notes — 21.4 From partition functions to equilibrium constants (module practical)

- Objective: derive K = Π(q°/N_A)^ν e^(−ΔE0/RT), compute K(T) for I₂ ⇌ 2I from NIST constants and D0, match JANAF within 3 %, get α(T, p), ΔrH° and ΔrS°, and rank the error sources.
- Likely confusion: the zero of energy. Students who used "lowest level = 0" for each species in 21.2–21.3 must now see that the species' zeros differ by D0; the Boltzmann factor carries that difference.
- Misconception: "statistical K is exact, JANAF is empirical" (or the reverse). JANAF ideal-gas tables are statistical calculations with fuller level sets; the comparison is model vs better model, not theory vs experiment. The experimental anchor is D0.
- Analogy: K as a lottery: the products win if they hold more tickets (states), but each product ticket costs D0, and the price in "Boltzmann units" falls as T rises.
