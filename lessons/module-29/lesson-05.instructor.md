# Instructor notes — 29.5 Metabolic pathways as chemistry (module practical)

- Objective: the learner reads glycolysis as ten organic reactions with named catalytic strategies, computes $\Delta G$ of each step in a red cell from $\Delta G^{\circ\prime}$ and concentrations, links far-from-equilibrium steps to regulation, and completes the module practical: weighted nonlinear MM against Lineweaver–Burk with a bootstrap, inhibition type of three inhibitors by global fits and AICc, and each inhibitor's effect when substrate builds up.
- Likely confusion: units in $Q$ (mol/L, matters when molecule numbers change); steps 6–10 occurring twice per glucose; "near equilibrium" meaning the net flux is a small difference of large forward and backward rates, not "no reaction".
- Misconception: "$\Delta G^{\circ\prime}$ tells which steps are irreversible" (aldolase). Second: "slightly positive $\Delta G$ means the step runs backwards". Third: "the most potent inhibitor at normal [S] is the best drug" (quiz q5).
- Analogy: a pathway is a canal with locks. Most locks are level (near equilibrium) and boats pass either way; at the three weirs (hexokinase, PFK-1, pyruvate kinase) water only falls, and the lock-keepers there decide how much traffic passes.
- Expected hands-on result: Part A $\Delta G$ (kJ/mol) −32.4, −1.2, −21.8, −1.1, +5.4, +8.1, −3.6, +1.0, +1.4, −21.1; steps 6+7 +4.5; total about −80 per glucose; with ATP 1.07 and ADP 0.30 mM steps 1, 3, 7, 10 shift by about 1.5 kJ/mol. Part B $V_\text{max}$ 117.0 ± 3.1, $K_m$ 0.485 ± 0.026 (nonlinear); LB 120.7, 0.514; bootstrap $K_m$ 0.435–0.541 vs 0.410–0.586 mM; X competitive $K_i$ 0.21 (ΔAICc 2.5), Y uncompetitive $K_i'$ 0.83 (2.5), Z mixed 0.29/1.37 (48); doses 0.32/2.48/0.39 mM; flux left at 2.5 mM 0.80/0.29/0.68. True values: V 120 µM/min, K 0.50 mM, X Ki 0.20, Y Ki' 0.80, Z 0.30/1.20 mM. Over 200 fresh noise copies the AICc rule picks the true model 89 % (X), 94 % (Y) and 100 % (Z) of the time; the misses choose mixed by a small margin.
- Extension: build a two-step pathway model (supply at constant rate, E1, then E2 with Michaelis–Menten kinetics), integrate it with `solve_ivp`, and watch [S] rise to its new steady state after each inhibitor is added; or regenerate the practical data with `py make_data.py 7` and see whether the conclusions hold.
