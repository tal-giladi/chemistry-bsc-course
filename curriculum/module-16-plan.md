# Module 16 plan — Organic Chemistry 2: Carbonyls, Aromatics and Synthesis

Technion: 01240711 Organic Chemistry 2. Depth: MIT OCW 5.13 Organic Chemistry II (Fall 2006) for scope; OpenStax Organic Chemistry (McMurry, 2023) chapters 15–25 as the reading text (sections checked 2026-10-17).
Simulations: none (outline §5). The virtual bench (`organic-bench`) comes in Module 24; spectra in Module 17.
Hands-on: mechanism and product drills with worked answers in `<details>`, RDKit 2026.9.1 (pinned in `labs/common/requirements.txt`) scripts in `labs/module-16/`, and a shared route checker (`org16.py`: a teaching library of ~25 classic reactions as reaction SMARTS, directing-effect and incompatibility checks, a starting-material catalogue). One kitchen activity (16.6: Maillard browning on toast, milk-brushed vs plain).
Data: `starting_materials.csv` (PubChem CIDs and SMILES), `carbonyl_data.csv` (hydration K orders of magnitude, pKa of α-H and acids from OpenStax 19.5, 20.4, 22.5), `amines_pka.csv` (OpenStax 24.3), `mutarotation_example.csv` (generated by `make_data.py` from OpenStax 25.5 rotations: α +112.2, β +18.7, equilibrium +52.6).
Module practical: lesson 16.7 Part B, "the final multi-step plan": two targets (1-(3-nitrophenyl)ethanol from benzene; 2-methyl-4-phenylbutan-2-ol, a fragrance alcohol, from catalogue compounds), retrosynthesis tree on paper, forward route as a route file, checked by `lesson07_practical.py`; plus a written "why this order" paragraph.
