# Module 03 plan — Linear Algebra and Differential Equations

Technion: 01040019 Linear Algebra M, 01040131 Ordinary Differential Equations (bridge scope only). Depth: MIT 18.06 (Strang, Spring 2010) and MIT 18.03 (Miller and Mattuck, Spring 2010) on OCW; Levitus, Mathematical Methods in Chemistry (LibreTexts) chapters 4, 5, 13, 15.
Simulations: kinetics (`first-order`, `consecutive`), oscillators (`single`, `damped`). Labs: `labs/module-03/` (numpy, scipy, matplotlib from `labs/common/requirements.txt`).
Module practical: lesson 05 Part B, A→B→C solved analytically, numerically (solve_ivp) and with the rate matrix's eigenvalues, then fitted to data.

| Lesson | Objectives (short) | Prerequisites | Hands-on | Sources to verify |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 03.1 Vectors, matrices, linear systems | Write a reaction as a composition matrix and balance it from the null space; solve a two-dye mixture from absorbances; judge an ill-conditioned system | 02.1 | `lesson01_linear.py`: balance KMnO4 + HCl, least-squares mixture from 13 wavelengths | OpenStax Chem 2e 4.1; LibreTexts two-component UV/Vis; scipy.linalg.null_space (1.18); MIT 18.06 |

| 03.3 First-order ODEs and rate laws | Separate variables for zero/first/second order; pick the order from linearised plots; integrate with Euler and solve_ivp and judge step error | 02.2, 02.3 | `lesson03_ode.py`: Euler error vs step, solve_ivp, order test on data | OpenStax Chem 2e 12.4; OpenStax Calc 2 4.3; scipy solve_ivp (1.18); MIT 18.03 |
