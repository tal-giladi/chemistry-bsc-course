# Module 22 plan — Chemical Kinetics

Technion: 01240414 Physical Chemistry: Chemical Kinetics (2.5 points); scope of the kinetics half of MIT 5.60 (plan.md 2.7). Depth: a first physical-chemistry kinetics course (Atkins/McQuarrie level) with real published data in every lesson.
Builds on: 02.1 (exp/log, Arrhenius linearised), 02.2 (rate as a derivative), 03.3 (integrated rate laws, Euler, solve_ivp), 03.5 (A → B → C, steady state, flip-flop), 05.3 (curve_fit and uncertainty), 13.4 (ΔG°, K(T)), 13.6 (K), 12.3 (tunnelling), 19.1 (Beer–Lambert), 21.4 (K from partition functions; written in parallel, linked by path). Forward: 23.4 (dye fading at home), 28 (Hammond, KIE), 29 (Michaelis–Menten in depth, inhibition), 32 (Butler–Volmer).
Simulation (outline §5): kinetics presets `first-order`, `second-order` (22.1), `consecutive` (22.3), `arrhenius` (22.4); `enzyme` (module 29's preset) also linked from 22.5.
Hands-on: Python labs in `labs/module-22/` (numpy, scipy, matplotlib only), helper `kin22.py`, pencil sets with answers in `<details>`, two kitchen experiments (22.4 effervescent tablet vs temperature; 22.5 yeast catalase with 3 % pharmacy hydrogen peroxide).
Real data:
- `invertase_timecourse.csv` — Michaelis & Menten (1913) time courses at five sucrose concentrations, fraction converted vs time, as tabulated in Johnson & Goody, Biochemistry 50, 8264 (2011), Table 1.
- `invertase_rotation.csv` — raw polarimeter readings of Michaelis & Menten Table I, experiments 1 (0.333 M) and 5 (0.0208 M), from the Johnson & Goody translation (Supporting Information).
- `invertase_initial_rates.csv` — the seven initial rates of Michaelis & Menten Table I (paper's arbitrary units).
- `oh_ch4_k.csv` — OH + CH4 → CH3 + H2O rate constants from two experimental studies listed in NIST SRD 17 (Gierczak et al. 1997, 196–420 K; Srinivasan et al. 2005, 840–2025 K), values computed from each study's fitted expression.
- N2O5 → NO2 + NO3 high- and low-pressure limits (IUPAC evaluation, via NIST SRD 17) and 2NO + O2 (IUPAC 2004, via NIST SRD 17) as constants in `kin22.py`.
Module practical: 22.5 Part B — fit the 1913 invertase data end to end: Michaelis–Menten fit of initial rates (Km vs 16.7 mM), then a global fit of all five time courses by numerical integration with and without product inhibition (Vmax vs 0.76 and 0.80 mM/min), residual analysis, lab-report template.
