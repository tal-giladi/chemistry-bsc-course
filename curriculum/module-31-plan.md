# Module 31 plan — Computational Chemistry Lab

Technion: 01260603 Advanced Lab in Computational Chemistry (3.0 points; plan.md S10, "done for real"). The learner does the same kind of work as in the real course on a laptop: molecular mechanics, conformer search, Hartree–Fock and DFT optimisations, calculated IR and NMR, transition states and TD-DFT excited states, with Avogadro 2.0.0, xtb 6.7.1 and ORCA 6.1.1 (versions, download pages and ORCA EULA checked 2026-10-17, pinned in `labs/module-31/README.md`).
Builds on: 12.2 (Hamiltonian as a matrix), 12.3 (NH3 tunnelling, 24 kJ/mol), 12.4 (harmonic oscillator, Born–Oppenheimer), 12.6 (variational principle, LCAO-MO), 15.2 (ethane 12 kJ/mol, butane MMFF94 scan, methylcyclohexane force-field warning), 17.2–17.4 (IR groups, 1H and 13C NMR shifts), 20.3 (fundamentals, anharmonicity), 20.4 (vertical transitions), 20.6 (labelling modes by symmetry), 22.4 (Eyring), 28.1 (PES, Hessian, one imaginary frequency). Module 30 is being written in parallel and is not linked.
Simulation: none (outline §5 lists none; the module is real software).
Hands-on: real runs. xtb inputs were run here with xtb 6.7.1pre (Windows build of the v6.7.1 release); ORCA was not run (it needs a personal forum registration and EULA acceptance), so ORCA inputs are untested and their "how you know it worked" values come from PySCF 2.14 runs of the same method and basis (water HF and B3LYP; CAM-B3LYP TDA polyenes) or from experiment. Python: `comp31.py` helpers, exercise + `*_solution.py` per lesson, numpy/scipy/matplotlib/RDKit from `labs/common/requirements.txt`. Small inputs and expected-output excerpts only.
Module practical: 31.6 Part C — calculated versus measured IR for six small molecules (H2O, NH3, CH4, CO2, H2CO, HCN): xtb (required) and ORCA r2SCAN-3c (optional), mode matching, least-squares scale factor, RMS error before and after scaling, lab report.
