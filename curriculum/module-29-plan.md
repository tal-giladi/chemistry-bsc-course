# Module 29 plan — Biochemistry and Enzymology

Technion: 01340019 Introduction to Biochemistry and Enzymology (2.5 points; plan.md 3.4: MIT 5.07SC Biological Chemistry I, Essigmann, Stubbe and Fedeles, Fall 2013). Depth: a first university biochemistry course seen by a chemist: protein thermodynamics, quantitative enzyme kinetics, mechanism, and metabolism as organic chemistry with free-energy bookkeeping.
Builds on: 06.2 (amino acids, pKa, folds, PDB, Mol*), 06.4 (ATP, ΔG = ΔG°' + RT ln Q, metabolism map), 05.3 (curve_fit and uncertainty), 13.4 and 13.6 (Gibbs energy, K), 15.4 (curved arrows, acids and bases), 16.2 and 16.3 (carbonyl addition, acyl substitution), 16.4 (aldol), 22.3 (SSA), 22.4 (TST), 22.5 (Michaelis–Menten, 1913 invertase data, Lineweaver–Burk warning; it promises inhibition and linear-plot pitfalls here), 26.3 (Hill, allostery), 26.4 (metal-ion catalysis). Module 28 lessons 28.1–28.3 exist (PES, Hammond, Hammett); 28.4–28.5 are being written in parallel, so only 28.1 and 28.2 are linked.
Simulation: kinetics (`enzyme`) in 29.2 and 29.3 (same preset as 22.5).
Hands-on: Python labs in `labs/module-29/` (numpy, scipy, matplotlib), helper `enz29.py`, `make_data.py` for generated sets, trimmed PDB 4CHA in `data/`, `fetch_pdb.py`; kitchen experiments in 29.1 (egg white at three temperatures) and 29.4 (pineapple bromelain and gelatin); pencil sets with answers in `<details>`.
Real data: 1913 invertase initial rates (Johnson & Goody 2011 translation); PDB 4CHA (Tsukada & Blow 1985); glycolysis ΔG°' (Jakubowski & Flatt, LibreTexts 13.1); red-cell metabolite levels (Protasov et al. 2019, Sci. Rep., CC BY). Generated (stated parameters): thermal and urea melts, invertase + fructose inhibition series (1913 constants), pH-rate profile, practical inhibitor set.
Module practical: 29.5 Part B — enzyme E1 with three candidate inhibitors: nonlinear MM fit against Lineweaver–Burk with a parametric bootstrap; global fits of competitive, uncompetitive and mixed models, AICc and LB patterns to decide the type; inhibition constants; pathway consequence when substrate builds up in front of the inhibited step; lab-report template.
