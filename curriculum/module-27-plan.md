# Module 27 plan — Electromagnetism and Matter

Technion: 01240416 Electromagnetism and Matter (plan.md 3.2): Maxwell's equations, multipoles, intermolecular forces, polarisability, dielectrics.
Depth: a second-year chemical-physics course, taught for chemists. Vector calculus only as much as needed (divergence and curl explained in words and with flux pictures); every equation ends in a number a chemist uses.
Builds on: 04.3 (Coulomb, field, potential, εr, dipole), 04.5 (light as an EM wave), 03.1 (vectors), 07.4 (polarity, IMF ranking), 12.6 (transition dipole), 20.1 (light–matter), 21.1 (Boltzmann factor), 13.6 (activity, solvents).
Simulations: none in outline §5 for this module. Hands-on = Python labs in `labs/module-27/`, pencil sets, and one safe home experiment (speed of light from a microwave oven and a plate of chocolate or cheese, 27.1).
Module practical: 27.4 Part B–C — from point charges to interaction energies: multipoles of water (TIP3P) and CO2, the water dimer energy scan, Keesom/Debye/London C6 for real molecules from CCCBDB μ and α and NIST ionisation energies, compared with boiling points; plus the Clausius–Mossotti/Debye analysis of 27.3 reused for α.
