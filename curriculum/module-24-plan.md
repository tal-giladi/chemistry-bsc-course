# Module 24 plan — Organic Chemistry Lab 1

Technion: 01240911 Organic Chemistry Lab 1, with the multi-step idea of 01240912 Organic Lab 2 (plan.md S6 + S9). Honest scope: this lab cannot be done at home. It needs organic solvents, corrosive reagents and a fume hood. The module replaces it with the virtual bench (`organic-bench`), the MIT Digital Lab Techniques Manual videos (the set MIT 5.301 uses), Python planning labs and two safe kitchen experiments (alum or sugar recrystallisation, paper chromatography of food colourings and markers).
Builds on: 01.3 (notebook, report), 01.4 (GHS, SDS, Israeli law, home rule), 07.4 (intermolecular forces, solubility), 08.4 (pKa), 13.x (phase equilibria, Raoult), 15.5 (SN2), 16.2 (Grignard), 16.3 (acyl substitution, aspirin), 16.4 (aldol), 16.7 (retrosynthesis), 17.2–17.5 (IR, NMR, unknowns), 19.3 (partition, multiple extractions, chromatography theory). Forward: 25 (instrumental lab), 28 (structure and reactivity).
Simulation (outline §5): organic-bench presets `techniques` (24.1, 24.2, 24.3), `aspirin` (24.4), `sn2`, `grignard`, `aldol` (24.5), `multistep` (24.6).
Videos (verified 2026-10-17): MIT OCW RES.5-0001 Digital Lab Techniques Manual, videos 3 (TLC basics), 4 (TLC advanced), 5 (work-up I: extraction, washing, drying), 6 (work-up II: rotary evaporator), 7 (filtration), 9 (recrystallisation), 12 (melting point), 15 (distillation I), 16 (distillation II), 17 (refluxing a reaction). 5.301 (IAP 2012) study-materials page lists 3, 5, 6, 9, 10, 11, 12, 15, 16, 17.
Labs: `labs/module-24/` (numpy, scipy, matplotlib, rdkit), helper `org24.py`, exercise + `_solution.py` per lesson, CSV templates for home data, illustrative observation file for the practical.
Classic teaching syntheses only: aspirin; 1-bromobutane from 1-butanol (SN2); triphenylmethanol (Grignard); dibenzalacetone (Claisen–Schmidt aldol); benzaldehyde → benzoin → benzil → benzilic acid (multi-step). No SDBS numbers anywhere: learners search SDBS by compound name; NIST WebBook links by ID.
Module practical: 24.6 — run the three-step sequence on the virtual bench, check every intermediate (yield, mp, IR, ¹H NMR), find and fix one impure intermediate, compute the overall yield and write it up on the lab-report template.
