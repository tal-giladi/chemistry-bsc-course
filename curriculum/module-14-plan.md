# Module 14 plan — Chemical Physics Lab: Oscillators and Signals

Technion: 01240611 Intro Lab in Chemical Physics (1.5 points). This is plan S4, done for real at home: harmonic and anharmonic oscillators, normal modes of a three-body system, time signals and computer processing, simple circuits, forced current, resonance width and Q factor, the vibrational spectrum of a triatomic molecule. Depth: MIT OCW 8.03SC Vibrations and Waves; OpenStax University Physics 1 ch. 15 and 2 ch. 15.
Simulations: oscillators (`single`, `damped`, `coupled-three`, `co2`), rlc (`resonance`). Labs: `labs/module-14/` (numpy, scipy, matplotlib from `labs/common/requirements.txt`; phone app phyphox 1.2.1, checked 2026-10-17).
Home electronics: battery or USB power only (phone on battery driving the circuit through its headphone output), with a WARNING in every lesson that uses it.
Module practical: 14.6, the RLC resonance curve, which uses the oscillator equation (14.1), damping and Q (14.2), the signal and frequency ideas (14.5) and a curve fit with uncertainty.
