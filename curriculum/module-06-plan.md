# Module 06 plan — Biology for Chemists

Technion: 01340127 Topics in Modern Biology (bridge scope only: the biology the chemistry modules need, seen through chemistry). Depth: MIT 7.012 Introduction to Biology on OCW; OpenStax Biology 2e.
Simulations: none. Labs: `labs/module-06/` (numpy and matplotlib from `labs/common/requirements.txt`; standard library `urllib` for the PDB download).
Module practical: lesson 06.4 Part B, "the ATP budget": a two-bottle yeast fermentation at home (mass loss → CO2 → ATP made), the free energy stored in that ATP under cellular conditions, and the H-bonds that hold ATP in a real enzyme (PDB 1ATP in Mol* and in Python). Uses membranes and compartments (06.1), protein structure and H-bonds (06.2), the phosphate chemistry shared with nucleic acids (06.3) and ATP thermodynamics (06.4).
