# Course details (for registering in the Academy)

- Suggested slug: `chemistry`
- GitHub repo: `tal-giladi/chemistry-bsc-course` (public)
- Free or paid: paid (Tal, 2026-10-18).
- Risk notice: This course includes optional home experiments with kitchen and pharmacy materials; follow each lesson's safety notes, never buy or use laboratory reagents, and never mix bleach with acids or ammonia. It is self-study and does not award a degree or train you to work safely at a real lab bench.
- One-line summary: The Technion B.Sc. in Chemistry, course by course, as a hands-on self-study path with simulated labs, real data and safe home experiments.
- Who it is for: adults who want a university-level understanding of chemistry at about 10–12 hours a week, starting from high-school maths and science.
- Format: 33 modules, 176 lessons, each with a hands-on part and a quiz; a module quiz per module; 17 interactive lab simulations; Python labs; a 180-hour research project.
- Prerequisites: high-school algebra and some high-school chemistry; a laptop; a phone for sensor experiments.
- What you will learn:
  - Use the maths, physics and Python a chemist needs, and analyse real data with honest uncertainty.
  - Explain bonding, structure, thermodynamics, equilibrium, kinetics and quantum chemistry and use them to predict.
  - Plan and run the analytical, physical and organic lab experiments of the degree in simulation, and analyse their data.
  - Identify unknown organic compounds from real MS, IR and NMR spectra.
  - Run real computational chemistry (xtb, ORCA) and compare it with experiment.
  - Read research papers and write lab reports and a research report.
  - Know exactly which bench skills you still lack and how to get them.
