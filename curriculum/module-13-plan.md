# Module 13 plan — Chemical Thermodynamics

Technion: 01240415 Physical Chemistry: Chemical Thermodynamics. Depth: MIT OCW 5.60 Thermodynamics and Kinetics (Spring 2008), lectures 1–24 (the thermodynamics part); LibreTexts Physical Chemistry for extra reading. This module removes the shortcuts of Module 8 (constant ΔH and ΔS, ideal everything) and gives the learner the tools to compute real numbers from NIST tables.
Simulations: calorimetry (`bomb`), gas-laws (`ideal`, `real`). Labs: `labs/module-13/` (numpy, scipy, matplotlib).
Data: `nist_shomate.csv` (NIST WebBook Shomate coefficients, Chase 1998 = NIST-JANAF; ΔfH°298 and S°298 CODATA Key Values as listed on the WebBook), `janaf_check.csv` (log Kf from the NIST-JANAF tables at janaf.nist.gov for checking), `phase_data.csv` (NIST WebBook phase-change data: Antoine coefficients, triple and critical points for H₂O and CO₂). Generated example data (`make_data.py`, seeded models): syringe compression, hot/cold mixing, freezing curves.
Module practical: lesson 13.6 Part B, "a thermodynamic data sheet for the water-gas shift": ΔrH(T), ΔrS(T), ΔrG(T) and K(T) from NIST data, checked against JANAF and against the constant-ΔH estimate from 08.3, equilibrium conversion, plus the home freezing-point measurement turned into a water activity. Uses 13.1 (first law, sign conventions), 13.2 (Cp, Kirchhoff), 13.3 (entropy from Cp), 13.4 (G, μ, K), 13.5 (phase equilibrium: freezing), 13.6 (activity).
