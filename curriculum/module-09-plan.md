# Module 09 plan — Analytical Chemistry 1: Measurement and Error

Planning file, not imported. Written 2026-10-17. Technion: 01240220 Analytical Chemistry 1 (experimental error, propagation, standard deviation, confidence intervals, linear regression, detection limits, titration theory, buffers, gravimetry). Depth: Harvey, Analytical Chemistry 2.1 (LibreTexts) chapters 4, 5, 6.8, 8 and 9.2; GUM (JCGM 100:2008) and its Monte Carlo supplement (JCGM 101:2008); NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook; Eurachem *Fitness for Purpose of Analytical Methods* (3rd ed., 2025); IUPAC Gold Book.
Simulations linked: `measurement-error` presets `repeat`, `bias` (09.1, 09.2), `propagation` (09.3); `titration` presets `weak-acid` (09.5), `buffer` (09.6).
Labs: `labs/module-09/` (numpy, scipy, matplotlib, uncertainties; Python 3.14, pins in `labs/common/requirements.txt`). Example data from `make_data.py` (seeded models, not measured).
Module practical: lesson 09.6 Part C, a full calibration with detection limit — a blue food-colour dilution series photographed with a phone, sRGB linearised, pseudo-absorbance, calibration line, residual check of the linear range, LOD and LOQ from blank photos, an unknown with its uncertainty and a check standard for bias. Example data provided; own photos optional.
