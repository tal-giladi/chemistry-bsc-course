# Module 12 plan — Quantum Theory in Chemistry

Technion: 01240408 Quantum Theory and its Applications in Chemistry (3.5 points). Depth: MIT 5.61 Physical Chemistry (Field, Fall 2017) on OCW; LibreTexts Physical Chemistry chapters 1–9; LibreTexts Zielinski et al., Quantum States of Atoms and Molecules (cyanine dyes, selection rules).
Simulations: quantum-box (`box`, `tunnel`, `oscillator`, `rotor`). Labs: `labs/module-12/` (numpy, scipy, matplotlib from `labs/common/requirements.txt`).
Module practical: the numerical Schrödinger solver. Learners write the finite-difference solver in 12.2, use the shared reference version `qm_solver.py` in 12.3–12.5, and in 12.6 (module practical) validate it, compute transition dipoles to derive selection rules, and fit the cyanine dye series.
