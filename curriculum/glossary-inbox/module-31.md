# Glossary inbox — Module 31

- **self-consistent field (SCF)** — the iterative procedure in which orbitals are recomputed in the field of all electrons until they stop changing; the core loop of Hartree–Fock and DFT programs (lessons/module-31/lesson-01.md)
- **model chemistry** — the full specification of a calculation: method, basis set, charge, spin multiplicity and key settings; energies are comparable only within one model chemistry (lessons/module-31/lesson-01.md)
- **hartree (Eh)** — the atomic unit of energy, 2625.50 kJ/mol or 27.2114 eV (lessons/module-31/lesson-01.md)
- **spin multiplicity** — 2S + 1 for total spin S: 1 for a closed-shell molecule, 2 for a radical, 3 for a triplet such as O₂ (lessons/module-31/lesson-01.md)
- **GFN2-xTB** — a semiempirical tight-binding quantum-chemistry method with fitted parameters for elements up to radon, run by the xtb program; fast enough for thousands of atoms (lessons/module-31/lesson-01.md)
- **imaginary frequency** — a vibrational mode with negative curvature, printed as a negative wavenumber; zero for a minimum, exactly one for a transition state (lessons/module-31/lesson-01.md)
- **force field** — a molecular-mechanics energy expression of bond, angle, torsion, van der Waals and electrostatic terms with fitted parameters, used without electrons (lessons/module-31/lesson-02.md)
- **MMFF94** — the Merck molecular force field (1996), parameterised for organic and drug-like molecules (lessons/module-31/lesson-02.md)
- **UFF** — the Universal Force Field, with rough rule-based parameters for the whole periodic table (lessons/module-31/lesson-02.md)
- **relaxed scan** — a series of optimisations in which one coordinate is held at stepped values and all others relax (lessons/module-31/lesson-02.md)
- **conformer search** — a systematic, stochastic or metadynamics exploration that finds the distinct low-energy conformers of a flexible molecule (lessons/module-31/lesson-02.md)
- **ETKDG** — RDKit's distance-geometry method for generating 3D conformers using experimental torsion preferences (lessons/module-31/lesson-02.md)
- **Hartree–Fock (HF)** — the mean-field method in which each electron moves in the average field of the others; it lacks electron correlation (lessons/module-31/lesson-03.md)
- **electron correlation** — the part of the electron–electron interaction missing from Hartree–Fock, recovered approximately by DFT, MP2 or coupled cluster (lessons/module-31/lesson-03.md)
- **density functional theory (DFT)** — methods that obtain the energy from the electron density with an approximate exchange–correlation functional (lessons/module-31/lesson-03.md)
- **exchange–correlation functional** — the approximate formula in DFT that turns the density into exchange and correlation energy, for example PBE, B3LYP or r2SCAN (lessons/module-31/lesson-03.md)
- **basis set** — the set of atom-centred functions used to build molecular orbitals, such as def2-SVP or def2-TZVP (lessons/module-31/lesson-03.md)
- **dispersion correction** — an added empirical term (D3, D4) that supplies the London dispersion attraction most functionals miss (lessons/module-31/lesson-03.md)
- **r2SCAN-3c** — a composite method combining the r2SCAN functional, a tailored triple-zeta basis, D4 dispersion and a basis-set superposition correction (lessons/module-31/lesson-03.md)
- **harmonic frequency** — a vibrational wavenumber from the second derivatives at the minimum, higher than the measured anharmonic fundamental (lessons/module-31/lesson-04.md)
- **scale factor** — a multiplicative correction, fitted by least squares for one method and basis, that brings harmonic frequencies close to measured fundamentals (lessons/module-31/lesson-04.md)
- **nuclear shielding** — the reduction of the magnetic field at a nucleus by its electrons, in ppm; the chemical shift is σ(TMS) − σ (lessons/module-31/lesson-04.md)
- **GIAO** — gauge-including atomic orbitals, the standard way to make calculated NMR shieldings independent of the coordinate origin (lessons/module-31/lesson-04.md)
- **zero-point energy (ZPE)** — the vibrational energy left at 0 K, half the sum of the real harmonic wavenumbers times hc (lessons/module-31/lesson-05.md)
- **nudged elastic band (NEB)** — a chain-of-structures method that relaxes a path between reactant and product to find the minimum-energy path and a transition-state guess (lessons/module-31/lesson-05.md)
- **intrinsic reaction coordinate (IRC)** — the steepest-descent path from a transition state down to the two minima it connects (lessons/module-31/lesson-05.md)
- **TD-DFT** — time-dependent density functional theory, which gives vertical excitation energies and oscillator strengths of excited states (lessons/module-31/lesson-06.md)
- **oscillator strength** — the dimensionless intensity of an electronic transition; near zero for a dark state (lessons/module-31/lesson-06.md)
- **Tamm–Dancoff approximation (TDA)** — a simplified TD-DFT that neglects de-excitation terms; ORCA's default for TD-DFT (lessons/module-31/lesson-06.md)
- **range-separated hybrid** — a functional whose share of exact exchange grows with electron–electron distance, such as CAM-B3LYP, better for charge-transfer and Rydberg-like states (lessons/module-31/lesson-06.md)
