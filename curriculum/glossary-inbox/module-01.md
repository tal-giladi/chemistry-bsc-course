- **credit point** — the Technion's unit of course weight, roughly one lecture hour a week for a semester (lessons/module-01/lesson-01.md)
- **practice testing** — recalling or solving without looking, such as quizzes and problems; one of the two best-supported study methods (lessons/module-01/lesson-01.md)
- **distributed practice** — spreading study and review over days and weeks instead of cramming; also called spaced review (lessons/module-01/lesson-01.md)
- **SI** — the International System of Units, with seven base units defined since 2019 by seven fixed constants (lessons/module-01/lesson-02.md)
- **mole** — the SI unit of amount of substance: exactly 6.022 140 76 × 10²³ specified particles (lessons/module-01/lesson-02.md)
- **Avogadro constant** — N_A = 6.022 140 76 × 10²³ mol⁻¹, exact by definition (lessons/module-01/lesson-02.md)
- **derived unit** — a unit built from SI base units, such as the newton, joule or pascal (lessons/module-01/lesson-02.md)
- **conversion factor** — a fraction equal to 1, such as 1000 mL / 1 L, used to change units without changing a quantity (lessons/module-01/lesson-02.md)
- **dimensional analysis** — using units to set up and check a calculation; both sides of an equation must have the same units (lessons/module-01/lesson-02.md)
- **significant figures** — the digits of a measured value that are known, plus the first uncertain one (lessons/module-01/lesson-02.md)
- **exact number** — a counted or defined number with unlimited significant figures, such as 5 repeats or 1000 mL per litre (lessons/module-01/lesson-02.md)
- **molarity** — concentration in moles of solute per litre of solution, symbol M (lessons/module-01/lesson-02.md)
- **lab notebook** — a permanent, dated record of work, written while the work is done (lessons/module-01/lesson-03.md)
- **raw data** — values exactly as read from an instrument, before any calculation (lessons/module-01/lesson-03.md)
- **processed data** — values calculated from raw data (lessons/module-01/lesson-03.md)
- **resolution** — the smallest step an instrument can show or a scale is marked in (lessons/module-01/lesson-03.md)
- **electronic lab notebook (ELN)** — software that keeps a lab notebook with timestamps and a change history (lessons/module-01/lesson-03.md)
- **lab report** — a structured account of one experiment: title, abstract, introduction, method, results, discussion, conclusion, references (lessons/module-01/lesson-03.md)
- **hazard** — what a substance can do to harm, a property of the substance (lessons/module-01/lesson-04.md)
- **risk** — how likely harm is in a real situation, given the hazard and the exposure (lessons/module-01/lesson-04.md)
- **exposure** — how much of a substance reaches a person, how concentrated, for how long and by which route (lessons/module-01/lesson-04.md)
- **GHS** — the UN Globally Harmonized System of Classification and Labelling of Chemicals (lessons/module-01/lesson-04.md)
- **signal word** — "Danger" (more severe) or "Warning" (less severe) on a GHS label (lessons/module-01/lesson-04.md)
- **hazard statement (H statement)** — a coded GHS sentence describing a hazard, such as H314 (lessons/module-01/lesson-04.md)
- **precautionary statement (P statement)** — a coded GHS sentence saying how to prevent or respond to harm, such as P280 (lessons/module-01/lesson-04.md)
- **safety data sheet (SDS)** — the 16-section document that describes a chemical's hazards, handling and emergency measures (lessons/module-01/lesson-04.md)
- **CID** — PubChem compound identifier, the number of a compound's PubChem page (lessons/module-01/lesson-04.md)
- **poisons permit** — the Israeli permit (היתר רעלים) required under the Hazardous Substances Law 1993 for businesses dealing in toxic substances (lessons/module-01/lesson-04.md)
- **density** — mass per unit volume, ρ = m/V (lessons/module-01/lesson-05.md)
- **random error** — error that changes from repeat to repeat; reduced by averaging (lessons/module-01/lesson-05.md)
- **systematic error** — error that is the same in every repeat (bias); found and removed only by calibration (lessons/module-01/lesson-05.md)
- **calibration** — measuring something with a known value to find and correct an instrument's bias (lessons/module-01/lesson-05.md)
- **precision** — how close repeated results are to each other (lessons/module-01/lesson-05.md)
- **accuracy** — how close a result is to the true value (lessons/module-01/lesson-05.md)
- **standard deviation** — s, the typical distance of single repeats from their mean (lessons/module-01/lesson-05.md)
- **standard uncertainty** — an uncertainty expressed as a standard deviation; for a mean of n repeats, s/√n (lessons/module-01/lesson-05.md)
- **Type A evaluation** — an uncertainty worked out statistically from repeated measurements (lessons/module-01/lesson-05.md)
- **Type B evaluation** — an uncertainty worked out from other knowledge, such as instrument resolution or a calibration certificate (lessons/module-01/lesson-05.md)
- **GUM** — the Guide to the Expression of Uncertainty in Measurement, JCGM 100:2008 (lessons/module-01/lesson-05.md)
