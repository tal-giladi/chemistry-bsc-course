- **burette** — a graduated glass tube with a tap that delivers a measured volume of titrant, read to 0.01 mL (lessons/module-10/lesson-01.md)
- **titrand** — the sample solution in the flask that the titrant is added to (lessons/module-10/lesson-01.md)
- **titration error** — the difference between end-point and equivalence-point volumes, as a percentage of the equivalence volume (lessons/module-10/lesson-01.md)
- **charge balance** — the condition that a solution is electrically neutral: total positive charge equals total negative charge (lessons/module-10/lesson-01.md)
- **primary standard** — a pure, stable, non-hygroscopic solid of known formula used to standardise a titrant, such as KHP for NaOH (lessons/module-10/lesson-01.md)
- **standardisation** — finding the exact concentration of a titrant by titrating a weighed primary standard (lessons/module-10/lesson-01.md)
- **potentiometric titration** — a titration followed with an indicator electrode and a reference electrode instead of a colour indicator (lessons/module-10/lesson-02.md)
- **Gran plot** — a linear transformation of titration data (for a weak acid, V × 10^-pH against V) whose x-intercept gives the equivalence volume (lessons/module-10/lesson-02.md)
- **formal potential (E°′)** — the measured potential of a redox couple when its oxidised and reduced forms have equal total concentrations in a stated medium (lessons/module-10/lesson-02.md)
- **redox indicator** — a compound whose oxidised and reduced forms have different colours, such as ferroin (lessons/module-10/lesson-02.md)
- **Mohr method** — direct titration of chloride with silver nitrate using chromate, which forms red Ag₂CrO₄ after equivalence (lessons/module-10/lesson-03.md)
- **Volhard method** — back titration of excess silver with thiocyanate in acid, with Fe³⁺ as indicator (lessons/module-10/lesson-03.md)
- **Fajans method** — precipitation titration with an adsorption indicator that colours the precipitate after equivalence (lessons/module-10/lesson-03.md)
- **chelating agent** — a ligand that binds one metal ion through several donor atoms at once, such as EDTA (lessons/module-10/lesson-03.md)
- **conditional formation constant** — a formation constant corrected for side reactions at a given pH, K′f = α(Y⁴⁻) Kf for EDTA (lessons/module-10/lesson-03.md)
- **metallochromic indicator** — a dye that changes colour when it binds a metal ion, used in EDTA titrations (lessons/module-10/lesson-03.md)
- **water hardness** — the total Ca²⁺ and Mg²⁺ in water, reported as mg/L of CaCO₃ (lessons/module-10/lesson-03.md)
- **gravimetric factor** — the ratio of the analyte's mass to the mass of the weighed form, such as Cl/AgCl = 0.2474 (lessons/module-10/lesson-04.md)
- **digestion** — keeping a precipitate hot in its solution so small crystals redissolve and larger, purer ones grow (lessons/module-10/lesson-04.md)
- **co-precipitation** — impurities carried down with a precipitate by inclusion, occlusion or surface adsorption (lessons/module-10/lesson-04.md)
- **constant mass** — the state reached when two successive weighings, after identical heating and cooling, agree within a set tolerance (lessons/module-10/lesson-04.md)
- **current efficiency** — the fraction of the charge passed that drives the intended electrode reaction (lessons/module-10/lesson-04.md)
- **coulometry** — determining an amount from the electric charge needed to convert it, using Faraday's law (lessons/module-10/lesson-04.md)
- **electrogravimetry** — depositing a metal on a weighed electrode by electrolysis and weighing the deposit (lessons/module-10/lesson-04.md)
- **ion-selective electrode (ISE)** — an electrode whose membrane potential responds mainly to one ion, following the Nernst equation (lessons/module-10/lesson-05.md)
- **selectivity coefficient** — a measure of how strongly an ISE responds to an interfering ion relative to its target ion (lessons/module-10/lesson-05.md)
- **TISAB** — total ionic strength adjustment buffer; fixes ionic strength and pH and frees complexed fluoride for fluoride ISE measurements (lessons/module-10/lesson-05.md)
- **standard addition** — adding a known amount of analyte to the sample itself so that matrix effects cancel (lessons/module-10/lesson-05.md)
- **indicator blank** — titrant needed to bring water plus indicator to the end-point colour, subtracted from each trial (lessons/module-10/lesson-06.md)
- **expanded uncertainty** — the combined standard uncertainty times a coverage factor, usually k = 2 for about 95 % coverage (lessons/module-10/lesson-06.md)
