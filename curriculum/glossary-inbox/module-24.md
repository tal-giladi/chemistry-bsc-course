# Glossary inbox — Module 24

- **reflux** — heating a reaction at the solvent's boiling point with a condenser on top, so the vapour condenses and runs back (lessons/module-24/lesson-01.md)
- **bumping** — sudden violent boiling of a superheated liquid; prevented with a stirrer bar or boiling chips added before heating (lessons/module-24/lesson-01.md)
- **separatory funnel** — a tapped funnel in which two immiscible liquids are shaken and then drained one at a time (lessons/module-24/lesson-01.md)
- **acid–base extraction** — moving an acid or base between organic and aqueous layers by changing the pH of the water (lessons/module-24/lesson-01.md)
- **emulsion** — a stable cloudy mixture of two immiscible liquids that will not separate into layers (lessons/module-24/lesson-01.md)
- **brine** — saturated aqueous sodium chloride, used to break emulsions and to pull water out of an organic layer (lessons/module-24/lesson-01.md)
- **drying agent** — an anhydrous salt, such as magnesium or sodium sulfate, that removes dissolved water from an organic solution by forming a hydrate (lessons/module-24/lesson-01.md)
- **rotary evaporator** — an apparatus that removes solvent under reduced pressure from a spinning, warmed flask (lessons/module-24/lesson-01.md)
- **recrystallisation** — purifying a solid by dissolving it in a minimum of hot solvent and letting crystals form on cooling (lessons/module-24/lesson-02.md)
- **mother liquor** — the solution left after crystals have formed; it holds the soluble impurities (lessons/module-24/lesson-02.md)
- **oiling out** — a compound separating from a hot solution as a liquid instead of crystals, usually because the solvent boils above its melting point (lessons/module-24/lesson-02.md)
- **second crop** — further crystals obtained by concentrating the mother liquor; usually less pure than the first (lessons/module-24/lesson-02.md)
- **theoretical plate** — one equilibrium stage of vaporisation and condensation in a distillation (lessons/module-24/lesson-02.md)
- **relative volatility** — the ratio of the vapour pressures of two liquids, α = P1*/P2*; the larger it is, the easier the distillation (lessons/module-24/lesson-02.md)
- **fractional distillation** — distillation through a column that provides many theoretical plates, for liquids with close boiling points (lessons/module-24/lesson-02.md)
- **azeotrope** — a liquid mixture that boils without changing composition and cannot be separated by ordinary distillation (lessons/module-24/lesson-02.md)
- **thin-layer chromatography (TLC)** — separation of compounds as an eluent climbs a thin layer of silica on a plate (lessons/module-24/lesson-03.md)
- **retention factor (Rf)** — distance moved by a spot divided by the distance moved by the solvent front (lessons/module-24/lesson-03.md)
- **eluent** — the solvent (mobile phase) that carries compounds along a TLC plate or column (lessons/module-24/lesson-03.md)
- **co-spot** — a TLC lane in which two samples are spotted on top of each other to test whether they are the same compound (lessons/module-24/lesson-03.md)
- **melting range** — the temperatures at which a solid starts and finishes melting; narrow for pure compounds, low and broad for impure ones (lessons/module-24/lesson-03.md)
- **mixed melting point** — the melting point of a sample ground with an authentic sample; unchanged if they are the same compound (lessons/module-24/lesson-03.md)
- **atom economy** — the percentage of the reactants' mass that ends up in the desired product (lessons/module-24/lesson-04.md)
- **iron(III) chloride test** — a colour test for phenols, which form a violet complex with Fe³⁺ (lessons/module-24/lesson-04.md)
- **limiting reagent** — the reagent that runs out first and sets the theoretical yield (lessons/module-24/lesson-04.md)
- **Claisen–Schmidt condensation** — a crossed aldol condensation between an aromatic aldehyde without α-hydrogens and a ketone (lessons/module-24/lesson-05.md)
- **umpolung** — reversal of the normal polarity of a carbon, as when thiamine makes an aldehyde carbon nucleophilic (lessons/module-24/lesson-06.md)
- **benzilic acid rearrangement** — conversion of a 1,2-diketone into an α-hydroxy acid by hydroxide, with a 1,2-shift of an aryl group (lessons/module-24/lesson-06.md)
- **overall yield** — the product of the step yields of a synthetic sequence (lessons/module-24/lesson-06.md)
