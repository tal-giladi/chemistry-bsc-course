- **centrifugal distortion constant (D)** — the small constant in F(J) = BJ(J+1) − DJ²(J+1)² that describes how a rotating bond stretches; for HCl about 5 × 10⁻⁴ cm⁻¹ (lessons/module-30/lesson-01.md)
- **isotopologue** — a molecule that differs from another only in the isotopes of its atoms, such as H³⁵Cl and H³⁷Cl (lessons/module-30/lesson-01.md)
- **overtone band** — a vibrational transition with Δv = 2 or more, allowed by anharmonicity; the HCl first overtone lies near 5668 cm⁻¹ (lessons/module-30/lesson-01.md)
- **pressure broadening** — widening of spectral lines by molecular collisions; it grows with gas pressure (lessons/module-30/lesson-01.md)
- **electron spin resonance (ESR, EPR)** — spectroscopy of unpaired electrons flipped by microwaves in a magnetic field; resonance at hν = gμ_B B (lessons/module-30/lesson-02.md)
- **g factor** — the dimensionless number that sets the resonance field of an unpaired electron; 2.0023 for a free electron, 2.002–2.006 for organic radicals (lessons/module-30/lesson-02.md)
- **hyperfine coupling constant (a)** — the splitting of an ESR line by a magnetic nucleus, in mT or MHz; it measures spin density at or near that nucleus (lessons/module-30/lesson-02.md)
- **Fermi contact interaction** — the hyperfine interaction from electron density at the nucleus itself; only s orbitals contribute (lessons/module-30/lesson-02.md)
- **McConnell relation** — a_H = Qρ, linking the proton coupling of a π radical to the spin density ρ on the attached carbon, |Q| ≈ 2.3 mT (lessons/module-30/lesson-02.md)
- **Breit–Rabi formula** — the exact energies of an electron spin coupled to a nuclear spin in any field; it gives the second-order shifts of large hyperfine patterns (lessons/module-30/lesson-02.md)
- **end correction** — the extra length, about 0.61 r for an open round pipe, by which the open end of a resonating tube acts longer than it is (lessons/module-30/lesson-03.md)
- **speed of sound (ideal gas)** — c = √(γRT/M); it gives the heat-capacity ratio γ = Mc²/RT (lessons/module-30/lesson-03.md)
- **free-electron (Kuhn) model** — the particle-in-a-box description of the π electrons of a conjugated dye, with N = p + 3 electrons in a box L = (p + 3 + α)ℓ (lessons/module-30/lesson-04.md)
- **bond alternation** — the pattern of short and long bonds in a polyene; it opens an energy gap that the flat box model lacks (lessons/module-30/lesson-04.md)
- **blind prediction** — a model prediction for data not used to fit the model; the strongest test of a model (lessons/module-30/lesson-04.md)
