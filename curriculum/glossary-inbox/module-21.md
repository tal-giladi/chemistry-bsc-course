- **microstate** — one complete specification of which energy level every molecule (or quantum) occupies; many microstates give the same measurable macrostate (lessons/module-21/lesson-01.md)
- **macrostate** — the state described by measurable quantities such as total energy, number of molecules and volume (lessons/module-21/lesson-01.md)
- **multiplicity (W)** — the number of microstates that belong to one macrostate; S = k_B ln W (lessons/module-21/lesson-01.md)
- **Einstein solid** — a model of N independent harmonic oscillators sharing identical energy quanta, used to count microstates (lessons/module-21/lesson-01.md)
- **statistical temperature** — the temperature defined by 1/T = (∂S/∂U) at constant V and N: how much entropy a system gains per unit of energy (lessons/module-21/lesson-01.md)
- **Boltzmann distribution** — the fraction of molecules in level i at temperature T, p_i = g_i e^(−E_i/k_BT)/q (lessons/module-21/lesson-01.md)
- **degeneracy (g)** — the number of distinct states that share one energy level, such as 2J + 1 for a rotational or atomic level (lessons/module-21/lesson-01.md)
- **partition function (q)** — the sum Σ g_i e^(−E_i/k_BT) over the levels of a molecule; roughly the number of thermally accessible states, from which all thermodynamic functions follow (lessons/module-21/lesson-02.md)
- **canonical partition function (Q)** — the partition function of a whole system of N molecules at fixed T and V; q^N for distinguishable and q^N/N! for indistinguishable (gas) molecules (lessons/module-21/lesson-02.md)
- **Schottky anomaly** — a peak in the heat capacity of a system with a small number of levels, caused by the upper level filling up to its high-temperature limit (lessons/module-21/lesson-02.md)
- **Einstein function** — the heat capacity of a harmonic oscillator, C_V/R = x² e^x/(e^x − 1)² with x = θ_vib/T (lessons/module-21/lesson-02.md)
- **vibrational temperature (θ_vib)** — hc ν̃ / k_B for a vibrational wavenumber ν̃; vibrations contribute little below it (lessons/module-21/lesson-02.md)
- **thermal wavelength (Λ)** — h/√(2πmk_BT), roughly the de Broglie wavelength of a molecule at temperature T; q_trans = V/Λ³ (lessons/module-21/lesson-03.md)
- **Sackur–Tetrode equation** — the translational entropy of an ideal gas, S = R[ln(q°_trans/N_A) + 5/2] (lessons/module-21/lesson-03.md)
- **rotational temperature (θ_rot)** — hcB/k_B for a rotational constant B; the high-temperature rotor formula needs T ≫ θ_rot (lessons/module-21/lesson-03.md)
- **symmetry number (σ)** — the number of rotations that turn a molecule into an indistinguishable copy of itself; it divides q_rot (2 for N₂, 12 for CH₄) (lessons/module-21/lesson-03.md)
- **D0 (dissociation energy from v = 0)** — the energy needed to separate a molecule in its lowest vibrational level into atoms; D_e minus the zero-point energy; the energy step in a statistical equilibrium constant (lessons/module-21/lesson-04.md)
- **degree of dissociation (α)** — the fraction of the starting molecules that are dissociated at equilibrium (lessons/module-21/lesson-04.md)
