# Module 18 plan — Inorganic Chemistry

Technion: 01240305 Inorganic Chemistry (2.5 points). Reading texts: OpenStax Chemistry 2e chapters 10.6, 18 and 19 (checked 2026-10-17) and the LibreTexts inorganic course pages linked per lesson. Depth: a first-degree inorganic course (main group, ionic solids, coordination chemistry, crystal and ligand field, colour and magnetism). Bonding in metalloproteins waits for Module 26; group theory for Module 20; band theory and XRD for Module 32.
Simulations: none (outline §5).
Hands-on: Python labs in `labs/module-18/` (numpy, matplotlib only, pinned in `labs/common/requirements.txt`), pencil sets with answers in `<details>`, two kitchen/pharmacy activities (18.1 red-cabbage test of an oxide, a hydroxide and a carbonate; 18.3 iron tablet in black tea, then lemon juice). Shared helper `inorg18.py` (CODATA 2022 constants, d-electron counting, the 24 rotations of an octahedron, colour wheel).
Data: `ionization_energies.csv` (copy of the Module 7 NIST ASD file, so the module zip stands alone), `born_haber.csv` (NIST WebBook/JANAF and CODATA ΔfH°, NIST ASD ionisation energies, measured electron affinities, lattice constants, Shannon radii), `complexes.csv` (Δo values and pairing energies from the sources listed in the lab README), `cards.csv` (the module-practical "complex cards": absorption maximum, measured μeff range mid-point).
Module practical: lesson 18.5 Part B — nine complex cards: from formula, absorption maximum and magnetic moment, deduce oxidation state, d count, geometry, spin state, CFSE, predicted colour and whether the band is d–d or charge transfer; checked by `lesson05_practical.py`; plus a one-page report with the lab-report template.
