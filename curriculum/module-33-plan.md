# Module 33 plan — Research Project

Technion: 01240353 Research Project in Chemistry (4 points; plan.md S11). Official SAP text (michael-maltsev/technion-sap-info-fetcher, courses_2025_200, read 2026-10-17): 180 hours of work in a research lab at an academic institution or an approved company, prior approval, a final report approved by the researcher the student worked with, handed in the semester after the work; requires 75 accumulated points. The poster belongs to 01240355 (Special Research Project, 6 points, about 240 hours in a Technion group: a proposal abstract approved by the supervisor before the start, a poster at a faculty forum and a final report). This course asks for both report and poster, as plan.md S11 does.
Honest scope: the real project is supervised; here the learner has no supervisor, no lab and no wet chemistry beyond the safe home set. The module teaches how to replace supervision as far as possible: a written proposal, validation against known values, a pilot, an hours log, and an outside academic reader.
Builds on: 01.3 (notebook and report), 05.5 (reproducible notebooks), 09.1–09.4 (error, statistics, propagation, calibration), 10.6 (home titration project), 11.1–11.3 (reading papers, judging sources, writing the report and figure), 31.1–31.4 (computational tools, calculated vs measured spectra; 31.5–31.6 are being written in parallel).
Simulation: none (outline §5).
Labs: `labs/module-33/` — `make_project.py` (project skeleton after Wilson et al. 2017), `lesson01_budget.py` (180-hour budget and machine-time feasibility), `lesson02_sample_size.py` (how many repeats or molecules for a target precision), `lesson03_poster_figure.py` (poster-sized parity figure with a conclusion title), `lesson04_package_check.py` (final package check); each with `*_solution.py`; `make_data.py` writes the generated demo data.
Projects: `projects/README.md` and seven briefs (three computational, one data, three safe home measurement).
Module practical: the 180-hour project itself, from proposal (33.1) to package check and outside reader (33.4).
