# Module 04 plan — Physics for Chemists

Technion: 01140077 Physics 1L (mechanics), 01140052 Physics 2 (electricity, magnetism, waves), bridge scope only. Depth: MIT 8.01SC, 8.02 and 8.03SC on OCW; OpenStax University Physics Vol. 1–3.
Simulations: oscillators (`single`) in 04.2, rlc (`dc`) in 04.4. Labs: `labs/module-04/` (numpy, matplotlib from `labs/common/requirements.txt`).
Module practical: lesson 05 Part B, Planck's constant from LED turn-on voltages (AA batteries, resistor, potentiometer, multimeter). Uses force/energy (04.1), waves (04.2), potential and the electronvolt (04.3), Ohm's law and circuits (04.4) and photons (04.5).
