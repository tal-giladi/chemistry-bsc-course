# Module 02 plan — Calculus for Chemists

Technion: 01040003 Calculus 1, 01040004 Calculus 2 (bridge scope only). Depth: MIT 18.01SC and 18.02SC on OCW; OpenStax Calculus Vol. 1–3.
Simulations: none. Labs: `labs/module-02/` (numpy, scipy, matplotlib from `labs/common/requirements.txt`).
Module practical: lesson 05 Part B, a home cooling curve analysed with numeric derivatives, integrals, a log fit and partial derivatives.

| Lesson | Objectives (short) | Prerequisites | Hands-on | Sources to verify |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 02.1 Functions: power, exp, log | Convert ln/log10; compute pH and half-life; linearise Arrhenius and read Ea from slope | 01.2 | `lesson01_arrhenius.py`: fit ln k vs 1/T on generated data, get Ea | OpenStax Chem 2e 12.5; OpenStax Calc 1 6.8; CODATA R (NIST); Kutschera 2019 (C-14) |
| 02.2 Derivatives as rates | Read a rate as a slope; apply power/exp/chain rules; estimate a derivative from data with central differences and judge noise | 02.1 | `lesson02_rates.py`: rate from concentration data, rate vs c gives k | OpenStax Calc 1 3.4; numpy.gradient docs (v2.5) |
| 02.3 Integrals as accumulated change | Integrate a rate to get an amount; compute isothermal work and heat from Cp(T); use trapezoid/Simpson on data | 02.2 | `lesson03_integrals.py`: work of expansion, N2 heat from NIST Shomate | OpenStax Calc 1 5.4; NIST WebBook N2 Shomate; scipy.integrate docs (v1.18) |
| 02.4 Series and approximations | Write first Taylor terms; apply small-x to weak acid; judge when the 5% rule fails | 02.1, 02.2 | `lesson04_small_x.py`: exact vs approximate pH over a Ka/C grid | OpenStax Calc 2 6.3; OpenStax Chem 2e 14.3 |
| 02.5 Several variables and partials | Compute partials of p(n,T,V); total differential for error estimates; module practical cooling curve | 02.2, 02.3, 02.4 | Part A pencil partials; Part B home cooling curve + `lesson05_cooling.py` | OpenStax Calc 3 4.3; Levitus (LibreTexts) ch. 8; NIST WebBook water Cp |

Data: all CSVs are generated by `labs/module-02/make_data.py` (seeded, parameters printed in the script) so they are realistic but not measured. Learners replace the cooling CSV with their own measurement.
