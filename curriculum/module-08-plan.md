# Module 08 plan — Foundations of Chemistry B: Energy, Equilibrium and Electrons

Technion: 01240118 Foundations of Chemistry B (lecture part plus the S2 lab part; the exact Technion experiments are not public). Depth: MIT 5.111SC Principles of Chemical Science; OpenStax Chemistry 2e chapters 5, 13, 14, 16 and 17.
Simulations: calorimetry (`coffee-cup`), titration (`strong-acid`). Labs: `labs/module-08/` (numpy, scipy, matplotlib from `labs/common/requirements.txt`).
Module practical: lesson 08.7, coffee-cup calorimetry of baking soda dissolving in water (cup calibrated by mixing hot and cold water, ΔT by extrapolation) and a first titration of vinegar with baking-soda solution and red-cabbage indicator (mass-based, 3 repeats). Uses thermochemistry and Hess (08.1), ΔG (08.2), equilibrium (08.3), acid–base and the cabbage indicator (08.4); redox and cells (08.5, 08.6) appear as the contrast case (an acid–base titration has no electron transfer) and in the extension.
