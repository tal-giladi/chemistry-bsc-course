# Module 32 plan — Electives: Electrochemistry and Solid State

Technion: 01270453 Electrochemistry: Principles and Applications (3.0) and 01270427 Solid State for Chemists, extended (3.5), the two faculty-list electives this course teaches in full (plan.md 3.5 (b)). Syllabi read from the official SAP text (michael-maltsev/technion-sap-info-fetcher, courses_2025_201 and courses_2025_200, read 2026-10-17): electrochemistry = interface, Nernst, Tafel, Butler–Volmer, Marcus, mass transport, voltammetry, industrial electrochemistry and energy storage; solid state = lattices, diffraction, reciprocal lattice, Drude and Sommerfeld, Bloch, bands, semiconductors, phonons.
Depth: third-year elective. Builds on 08.6 (Nernst, cells), 19.5 and 25.6 (CV, Nicholson k0), 22.4 (Arrhenius and TST), 18.2 (packing, lattice constant, density), 12.6 (MOs from AOs), 03.2 (eigenproblems), 04.5 (LED turn-on voltage), 20.5 (point groups).
Simulation (outline §5, built later): voltammetry `butler-volmer` (32.1).
Data: Tafel and corrosion curves are generated (seeded, `make_data.py`), stated in the lessons. Powder XRD patterns are REAL: Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.18522088 (Dahiya, Dahiya and Sabarwal 2026, CC BY 4.0): CdO, In2O3 and a CdO–In2O3 composite, Rigaku MiniFlex, Cu target, no Kβ filter (header of the .asc file). Converted to CSV with attribution and a note of the changes. References: COD 9006671 (CdO, a = 4.6963 Å); In2O3 bixbyite a ≈ 10.117 Å (COD, verify in lesson). Cu Kα1, Kα2, Kβ wavelengths from NIST X-ray transition database.
Module practical: 32.5 Part A — phase analysis of the real CdO–In2O3 composite (indexing, shifts, Kβ, unassigned peaks, Scherrer sizes) plus a coated-steel corrosion verdict from Tafel fits; one report. Part B is the electives guide.
