# Module 30 plan — Physical Chemistry Lab 2

Technion: 01240613 Physical Chemistry Lab 2 (plan.md S8): advanced spectroscopy (IR, UV-Vis, ESR) and thermal/acoustic measurements. This course picks it over Organic Lab 2 because most of it can be simulated from first principles.
Depth: a third-year physical chemistry lab. Each lesson = one classic experiment: the physics from first principles, the procedure (why each step, safety, what goes wrong), a simulation run (built later), a Python analysis in `labs/module-30/`, and the plan.md S8 home experiment where one exists.
Builds on: 12.2 (particle in a box, cyanines), 12.4 (anharmonic oscillator), 12.5 (rotor, angular momentum, hydrogen atom), 12.6 (dye fit), 14.5 (phone FFT, bottle resonance), 17.3 (NMR splitting, n+1), 18.5 (unpaired electrons), 19.1 (Beer–Lambert), 20.2–20.4 (rotational, rovibrational, electronic spectra; HCl NIST band), 21.1 and 21.3 (Boltzmann populations, frozen vibration), 23.1 (gamma by Clément–Desormes), 25.1 (phone spectrometer), 09.3 (propagation).
Simulations (outline §5, built later): ir-esr `hcl` (30.1), `esr-methyl` and `esr-hydrogen` (30.2); quantum-box `dye` (30.4). 30.3 has no simulation: home experiment plus Python.
Home experiments: 30.3 speed of sound in a tube closed at one end, phone microphone + phyphox Audio Spectrum, several tube lengths (plan.md S8). 30.4 optional kitchen extension: carrot and tomato pigments in cooking oil (polyenes, a contrast to cyanines), with the 25.1 phone spectrometer if built. 30.1 and 30.2 have no home experiment (HCl gas and an ESR magnet are not home equipment).
Module practical: 30.4 Part C — a full report on the conjugated-dye series: two box models fitted to published λmax values, a blind prediction tested on a fourth dye, the polyene failure, and a one-paragraph comparison with the HCl and ESR models of the module.

Data: `labs/module-30/make_data.py` generates the example files (seeded): the HCl spectrum from NIST WebBook constants (not a measured spectrum; stated in the lesson), ESR derivative spectra from published constants, a tube-resonance series. Safety: 30.1 HCl gas (H280, H314, H331) — lab only, never at home; 30.2 strong magnets and microwaves — lab only; 30.3 CAUTION: moderate volume, keep the phone away from ears, cut tubes away from the body; 30.4 cyanine dyes and methanol are lab reagents — not at home; the kitchen extension uses only carrot, tomato paste and cooking oil, no heating of oil.
