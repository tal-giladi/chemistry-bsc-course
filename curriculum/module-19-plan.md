# Module 19 plan — Analytical Chemistry 2: Instrumental Methods

Technion: 01240213 Analytical Chemistry 2 (spectrophotometry, flame and plasma methods, fluorimetry, extraction, GC and LC, ion exchange, electroanalysis, cyclic voltammetry). Depth: Harvey, *Analytical Chemistry 2.1* (LibreTexts), chapters 7.7, 10, 11, 12.
Simulations (built later): `spectrometer` presets `beer-lambert`, `stray-light` (19.1); `chromatography` presets `van-deemter` (19.3), `gc` (19.4); `voltammetry` preset `reversible` (19.5).
Hands-on: Python labs in `labs/module-19/` (numpy, scipy, matplotlib only), all data simulated by `make_data.py` from the lesson equations; NIST WebBook gas-chromatography pages for limonene and linalool retention indices (19.4). No SDBS. No home experiments with reagents; the tonic-water and phone-spectrometer experiments belong to Module 25 and are only pointed to.
Module practical: 19.5 Part C — HPLC-UV method for caffeine in an energy drink: van Deemter fits for two columns under a pump-pressure limit, resolution by the Purnell equation, calibration with LOD/LOQ (09.4), spike recovery, label and EU-warning check. Template: `templates/lab-report.md`.
