# Module 28 plan — Structure and Reactivity in Organic Chemistry

Technion: 01240703 Structure and Reactivity in Organic Chemistry (2.5 points; plan.md 3.3: potential energy surfaces, reaction mechanisms, Hammond postulate, Hammett equation, reactive intermediates). Depth: an introductory physical-organic course (Anslyn and Dougherty / Carroll level), built on the organic modules 15 and 16 and the kinetics of Module 22.
Builds on: 15.2 (conformations, Boltzmann populations, ring flip), 15.4 (curved arrows, pKa, ARIO), 15.5 (SN1/SN2, carbocations), 15.6 (E1/E2), 16.5 (EAS, arenium ion, directing effects), 12.3 (tunnelling), 12.4 (zero-point energy, isotopes), 13.4 (ΔG and K), 21.1 (Boltzmann distribution), 22.3 (mechanisms, rate-determining step, SSA), 22.4 (transition-state theory, Eyring), 26.4 (Marcus theory), 05.3 (fits with uncertainty). Forward: 29 (enzyme mechanisms), 31 (computational chemistry: optimising minima and transition states).
Simulations: none for this module (outline §5 lists "—").
Hands-on: Python labs in `labs/module-28/` (numpy, scipy, matplotlib; rdkit only in `fetch_iupac_pka.py`), helper `sr28.py`, pencil sets with answers in `<details>`. No home experiment: nothing in this module can be measured safely in a kitchen with useful precision, so every lesson uses published data or a model surface.
Real data:
- `diazonium_coupling.csv` — second-order rate constants for 3-aminothiophene + eight para-substituted benzenediazonium ions, 50 % H2O–DMSO, 20 °C, with H/D data for three of them: El Abed et al., ChemPhysChem 26, e202500553 (2025), Table 1, CC BY 4.0 (PMC12710183, checked 2026-10-17).
- `sigma_hansch.csv` — σm, σp, σp+ for 12 substituents, Hansch, Leo and Taft, Chem. Rev. 91, 165 (1991), via the Zipse (LMU) table and the El Abed Table 1.
- IUPAC Digitized pKa Dataset v2.4 (CC BY-NC 4.0, so not copied into the repo): `fetch_iupac_pka.py` downloads it to `data/downloaded/` (gitignored) and extracts substituted benzoic acids, phenols and anilinium ions in water at 25 °C.
- C–H bond dissociation enthalpies (Blanksby and Ellison 2003 via Wenthold, LibreTexts); H–Cl, H–Br from NIST WebBook ΔfH.
- Propane halogenation product ratios at 25 °C (LibreTexts, Morsch and Andrews 15.06): Cl 45:55, Br 3:97.
Module practical: 28.5 Part C — a mechanism case file on the diazonium coupling: Hammett plot with σp (curved) and σp+ (straight), ρ with uncertainty against the authors' 2.48, Yukawa–Tsuno r, H/D isotope effects, then a reasoned energy profile (rate-determining step, Wheland intermediate, early or late transition state); lab-report template.
