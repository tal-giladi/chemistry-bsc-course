# Module 23 plan — Physical Chemistry Lab 1

Technion: 01240610 Physical Chemistry Lab 1 (plan.md S5): thermodynamics, kinetic theory of gases, thermochemistry, chemical equilibrium, electrochemistry and potentiometry, kinetics, spectrophotometry, surface chemistry.
Depth: a second-year physical chemistry lab. Each lesson = one lab topic: the classic experiment explained (procedure, why each step, safety, what goes wrong, data and error analysis), a simulation run (built later), a Python analysis in `labs/module-23/`, and the plan.md S5 home experiment where one exists.
Builds on: 07.6 (gases), 08.1 and 08.7 (calorimetry, extrapolation), 08.3 (equilibrium), 08.6 (cells, Nernst, lemon cell), 09.3 (propagation), 09.6 (phone-photo colour method), 13.1–13.2 and 13.6 (first law, enthalpy, activity), 19.1 (Beer–Lambert), 22.1–22.2 (rate laws, isolation, measuring rates).
Simulations (outline §5, built later): gas-laws `kinetic` (23.1); calorimetry `neutralisation`, `dissolution` (23.2); spectrometer `equilibrium` (23.3); kinetics `dye-fading` (23.4). 23.5 and 23.6 have no simulation: home experiment plus Python.
Home experiments exactly as plan.md S5: coffee-cup calorimeter with citric acid and baking soda (cold) and dissolving salts (23.2); food dye fading in dilute household bleach with phone-camera colour readings (23.4, bleach WARNING: never mix with acids or ammonia, small amounts, good airflow); lemon or salt-water Zn/Cu battery with a multimeter (23.5); drop counting with more and more detergent (23.6). 23.1 and 23.3 have no home experiment (none in S5): simulation, Python and pencil.
Module practical: 23.6 Part C — surface tension against detergent concentration with replicates, propagated uncertainty, a two-line fit for the break (effective CMC), the Gibbs surface excess and area per molecule, written up with the lab-report template.
