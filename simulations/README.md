# Simulations

Standalone educational simulations of the course's labs (plain HTML, CSS and classic scripts; no network). Each is an educational model: it teaches the procedure, why each step is done, the safety reasoning, what can go wrong and how to analyse the data, but it does not train your hands. Pass `?preset=<name>` to start at a preset. Shared code lives in `common/`.

- [measurement-error](measurement-error/index.html) — repeat readings, 95 % intervals, bias and calibration with a CRM, propagation (first order and Monte Carlo). Lessons 01.5, 09.1, 09.2, 09.3. Presets: `repeat`, `bias`, `propagation`.
- [glassware](glassware/index.html) — weighing by difference on a drifting balance, a dilution with its uncertainty budget, parallax at a meniscus. Lesson 07.7. Presets: `weighing`, `dilution`, `parallax`.
- [gas-laws](gas-laws/index.html) — ideal gas and work of compression, van der Waals Z and isotherms with the Maxwell construction, a hard-sphere kinetic box, a Clément–Desormes bench. Lessons 07.6, 13.1, 13.5, 23.1. Presets: `ideal`, `real`, `kinetic`.
- [calorimetry](calorimetry/index.html) — coffee-cup calibration, enthalpy of solution and of neutralisation, bomb calorimeter, all with fore- and after-line extrapolation. Lessons 08.1, 08.7, 13.2, 23.2. Presets: `coffee-cup`, `dissolution`, `neutralisation`, `bomb`.
- [titration](titration/index.html) — a burette bench with parallax, drops, an overshooting tap, rinsing and air-bubble mistakes: acid–base with five indicators and KHP standardisation, a pH electrode with two-buffer calibration, ageing, lag and drift (derivative and Gran end points), buffer capacity, Fe²⁺/Ce⁴⁺ with ferroin or a Pt electrode, Mohr chloride with a reagent blank, EDTA hardness at several pH values, and colorimetric manganese in steel. Lessons 08.7, 09.5, 09.6, 10.1, 10.2, 10.3, 10.5, 10.6. Presets: `strong-acid`, `weak-acid`, `indicator-choice`, `buffer`, `potentiometric`, `redox`, `precipitation`, `edta`.
- [gravimetry](gravimetry/index.html) — chloride as AgCl with filtering, peptisation, co-precipitation and drying-to-constant-mass choices; copper by electrodeposition at controlled potential or current (current efficiency, completeness test); a coulometric iodine titration of vitamin C with a blank; baking soda heated to constant mass. Lesson 10.4. Presets: `precipitate`, `electrogravimetry`, `coulometry`, `oven`.
- [kinetics](kinetics/index.html) — integrated rate laws and the order test with Euler's method, A → B → C with the steady-state approximation, Arrhenius and Eyring fits, dye fading in dilute bleach (pseudo-first-order, A∞, temperature drift), Michaelis–Menten with inhibition and assay design. Lessons 03.3, 03.5, 22.1, 22.3, 22.4, 22.5, 23.4, 29.2, 29.3. Presets: `first-order`, `second-order`, `consecutive`, `arrhenius`, `dye-fading`, `enzyme`.
- [oscillators](oscillators/index.html) — a mass on a spring measured statically and dynamically (stopwatch or phyphox, spring's own mass), a damped ringdown recorded like a phone accelerometer (peak method, Q, quadratic drag, anharmonic drift), three coupled masses or pendulums (normal modes, beats, mode timing fit), and CO₂ normal modes with a valence force field, isotopes and IR activity. Lessons 03.4, 04.2, 14.1, 14.2, 14.3, 14.4. Presets: `single`, `damped`, `coupled-three`, `co2`.
- [rlc](rlc/index.html) — a DC resistor ladder read with a 3½-digit multimeter (tolerances, tired cells, meter loading, the current-range short circuit), and a series RLC resonance curve driven by a phone tone (fixed vs auto range, overload, breadboard faults, coil losses, weighted fit of f₀, Q and A). Lessons 04.4, 14.6. Presets: `dc`, `resonance`.
- [quantum-box](quantum-box/index.html) — a finite-difference Schrödinger solver: particle in a box (finite walls, convergence), tunnelling through a barrier and the ammonia double well, harmonic and Morse HCl/DCl, rigid-rotor populations and the hydrogen radial equation, and Kuhn's cyanine and polyene dye series with calibration, fit and blind test. Lessons 12.2, 12.3, 12.4, 12.5, 30.4. Presets: `box`, `tunnel`, `oscillator`, `rotor`, `dye`.
- [boltzmann](boltzmann/index.html) — two-level populations and the Schottky peak with a counting experiment that recovers the gap, the harmonic or anharmonic ladder (q, C_V, S), the rotational partition function with symmetry numbers and nuclear-spin statistics, and microstates of Einstein solids with the coin exchange game. Lessons 21.1, 21.2, 21.3. Presets: `two-level`, `ladder`, `rotor`, `microstates`.
- [spectrometer](spectrometer/index.html) — a UV-Vis bench (slit bandwidth, stray light and its ceiling, cuvette material, zeroing, photometric error curve, dilution into range), two dyes by two wavelengths and full-spectrum least squares (ill-conditioned wavelengths, turbidity, an undeclared dye), the FeSCN²⁺ equilibrium constant with matched blanks, the incomplete-complexation correction and a Job plot, quinine fluorescence (inner filter, chloride quenching, external calibration against standard addition), and flame AAS of calcium (lamp warm-up, burner height, La, ionisation, background correction, spike, check standards). Lessons 19.1, 23.3, 25.1, 25.2, 25.3. Presets: `beer-lambert`, `stray-light`, `equilibrium`, `mixture`, `fluorescence`, `aas`.
- [chromatography](chromatography/index.html) — temperature-programmed capillary GC (carrier gas and velocity, split and overload, DB-5 or wax, alkane ladder and retention indices, FID or MS with solvent delay and an m/z 119 trace), reversed-phase HPLC method building (linear solvent strength, pH near a pKa, acetonitrile or methanol, gradient, pressure limit, system suitability on a new and an aged column), and van Deemter measurements with a fit for packed, capillary and HPLC columns. Lessons 19.3, 19.4, 25.4, 25.5. Presets: `van-deemter`, `gc`, `temperature-program`, `hplc`, `overlap`.
- [voltammetry](voltammetry/index.html) — cyclic voltammetry by finite differences (cell preparation mistakes: oxygen, no electrolyte, fouled electrode, blocked reference; scan-rate series, adsorbed species, uncompensated resistance, Randles–Ševčík, Nicholson's ψ and k⁰), and Butler–Volmer polarisation curves with Tafel and micro-polarisation fits, iR correction and a mass-transport limit. Lessons 19.5, 25.6, 32.1. Presets: `reversible`, `scan-rate`, `quasi`, `butler-volmer`.
- [nmr](nmr/index.html) — a ¹H NMR spectrometer that solves the spin Hamiltonian exactly (n+1 patterns, roofing and second-order spectra as the field drops), with field, shimming, scans, wet solvent, peak picking, integrals, a click-to-measure ruler and eight unknowns to identify. Lessons 17.3, 17.5. Presets: `ethyl`, `isopropyl`, `second-order`, `unknown`.
- [ir-esr](ir-esr/index.html) — the HCl rotation-vibration band from NIST constants (both isotopes, fundamental and overtone, temperature, pressure broadening, saturation, resolution, centrifugal distortion; peak fit and Boltzmann temperature), and ESR derivative spectra of radicals (2nI + 1 patterns, deuteration, modulation and power) and of the hydrogen atom with exact Breit–Rabi fields. Lessons 20.3, 30.1, 30.2. Presets: `hcl`, `esr-methyl`, `esr-hydrogen`.
- [organic-bench](organic-bench/index.html) — technique stations (reflux, extraction and drying, recrystallisation, distillation, TLC, melting point) and step-by-step syntheses with choices and realistic failures (bumping, emulsions, the wrong layer, oiling out, wet product, low yield): aspirin, 1-bromobutane, a Grignard, an aldol and the benzoin → benzil → benzilic acid sequence, each ending with yield, melting point, TLC and IR/¹H NMR key peaks. Lessons 24.1–24.6. Presets: `techniques`, `aspirin`, `sn2`, `grignard`, `aldol`, `multistep`.
- [common](common/) — shared `sim.css` (light/dark tokens, layout) and `sim.js` (label, query and presets, seeded RNG, safe storage, statistics, CSV export, canvas plot, animation loop; API documented at the top of the file).
