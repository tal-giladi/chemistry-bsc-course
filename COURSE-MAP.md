## Fast route (about 18 months)

If you only want to understand chemistry, not cover the whole degree: Modules 1, 7, 8, 9, 10, 13, 15, 16, 17, 19, 20, 22, 23, 24 and 25.

## Pace

At 10–12 hours a week the full course takes about 5.5–6 years; at 18–20 hours about 3.5–4 years. If you already know the maths and physics, skip Modules 2–4 after passing their module quizzes.
